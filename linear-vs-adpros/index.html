    Linear vs AdPros: Competitive Deep-Dive

    wearelinear.com teardown · July 1, 2026 · Internal use only

    Competitive Intelligence

    They win on distribution, not on their website.

    Linear's 30+ monthly meetings do not come from wearelinear.com (~1,400 visits/month, 65% bounce). They run a founder-led machine: daily LinkedIn on two cross-posting accounts, comment-gated lead magnets, free live trainings, cold email, and agency referrals. The site is a 6-page closing asset. And their homepage proof section appears to be lifted from adpros.com copy.

    01 PROFILE

    Who They Are

    02 PIPELINE

    The Acquisition Machine

    Ciaran Finn, verbatim on LinkedIn (May 24): "The only 3 channels we use for client acquisition are: LinkedIn, Cold Email, Agency Referrals." The "30 meetings from cold email" story is really this blended system.

    1

    Two founder accounts, daily posts, cross-posted

    Identical posts published on both accounts, each reposts the other. Evan at 38K followers. Content splits between DTC tactics (for brand clients) and agency-building (for the coaching funnel).

    2

    Comment-gated lead magnets

    "Connect with me + comment TEMPLATE / STATIC / COPY / SCALE / CLIENT and I'll send it." Every magnet forces a connection (compounding reach) and opens a DM thread (pipeline). These posts pull 100 to 350 comments each.

    3

    Cold email, run quietly

    Stated as one of the three core channels. They never publish the playbook; they sell it as a gated "40-minute breakdown" video.

    4

    Free live Zoom trainings

    ~120 attendees on June 15, with a 48-hour scarcity replay. Nurtures brand prospects and feeds the agency-owner coaching funnel at the same time.

    5

    Agency referral partnerships

    Non-competing agencies passing deals. Channel three of three. One leaked creative title ("Ciaran BOF IG story sequence") also suggests they retarget their own funnel with Instagram story ads (unconfirmed).

    06 ASSESSMENT

    Strengths vs Weaknesses

    Where they're strong

    • Repeatable pipeline independent of paid and SEO; the gated-post engagement (100 to 350 comments) is real
    • Creative-volume offer matches Meta's Andromeda-era demands
    • High-margin service design (70%+ gross claimed) funds aggressive growth
    • Named-brand halo: Drew Barrymore, Loop, Honeylove
    • Tight ICP and Meta-first focus

    Where they're exposed

    • Zero inbound assets: no SEO, no blog, no list moat, 65% bounce
    • Rebrand debris: "Work At BFM" titles, BFM/YGU testimonials, Webflow project still named "blufinnmedia-updates"
    • Anonymous case studies, unverifiable spend claims, inconsistent numbers (200+ vs 250+ brands)
    • Guru funnel: selling "how to run an agency" while selling agency services reads as split focus to 8-9 figure operators
    • Key-person risk: the pipeline IS two personal LinkedIn accounts
    07 ACTION

    Plays for AdPros

    1

    Wrap the founder-content flywheel around the cold email engine we already run

    Daily cadence on Nehal's account, comment-gated magnets, cross-account amplification. That is the one piece of their machine AdPros doesn't run yet.

    2

    Gate existing assets

    Named case studies, training content, and the Claude/AI workflows are stronger magnets than anything Linear gives away.

    3

    One live training per month

    The edu/training infrastructure already exists. 120 attendees plus a scarcity replay is their whole accelerant.

    4

    Exploit the proof asymmetry

    In head-to-head deals: "ask them for the brand name behind the case study." AdPros has named, linkable proof.

    5

    Own the AI story

    They mock AI creative in posts while claiming "AI creative systems" on the site. AdPros' Claude-native operation is a differentiated position neither side of their messaging occupies.

    6

    Watch for inbox collisions

    Both firms cold-email the same scaling-ecommerce pool. Monitor their targeting and keep differentiating the AdPros homepage so prospects seeing both never read them as interchangeable.

    Watch list Their hiring page (3 open delivery roles) and the "$500K/mo by end of 2026" target: if headcount jumps, the outbound machine is converting. Also watch whether the coaching funnel grows into a full info-product business or funds more aggressive client acquisition.
