02What Moved the Needle

Key actions, tests, and campaign changes this week

Creative Batch Diversity

A ton of batches have been launched and are currently in rotation allowing for good creative diversity and volume of ads to optimize on. This has sustained results despite removing some best performers due to the Emori creator situation.

Best Performers Spend Share

Best Performers ad set continues to increase in percentage of overall campaign’s budget — brought up from the 40% range to currently 53% in last 7 days. This is the best ncROAS campaign at scale.

WHAT WORKED THIS WEEK — AND WHY

  • 2-Step Treatment Stabilized: Back again at 110% ncROAS L7, although at slightly less spend. Will be adjusting minimum spends.
  • 2-Step Creative Wins (not just AOV): Performance isn’t due to higher ncAOV ($72 vs $68 account avg) — more so the creative crushing it.
  • Even More Clicks (35k vs 31k): More fresh blood with high NVPs entering the funnel. Could be cheaper traffic — interesting to monitor.

WHAT DIDN'T WORK — AND WHY

  • Emori’s Ads Shut Off — Major Impact: Had to shut off best performing ads, bringing ncROAS down by over 20 percentage points. She was always top 2 most spending across multiple funnels.
  • Cost Cap Crashed (100% → 58%): Major hit due to Emori’s ads being shut off — clearly very effective as BOF content.
  • Quiz Funnel Hit: Went from 2nd highest spending funnel to 4th, with lower ncROAS after Emori’s ad removal. Slow progress on expansion.
  • Budget Expansion Halted: Haven’t been able to expand budget across account due to current state.
