    03 Your role

    Who we're looking for

    You're a practical dog owner who's been defaulting to whatever bag looks familiar. Curious rather than angry — you found something out and you're passing it along.

    The energy

    Talking head to camera with green-screen b-roll behind you. Plain-spoken, informational, no hard sell.

    Awareness stage

    Solution-aware, mechanism-unaware. They've never asked why working animals get something different.

    Creative type

    Myth vs Fact · Green-Screen Video. Angle: What Farmers Knew (Piglet Story).

    04 The angle

    Your concept

    “What The Farms Figured Out First” — working animals get plasma, your joint chew gets glucosamine. Not “here's a new ingredient.” It's the gap: farmers have used plasma on working animals for over 40 years, and the pet aisle still leads with glucosamine. WINPRO put what the farms already knew into a daily chew.

    Concept C151Unique mechanism: 40 years, 300+ studies · Avatar: budget-conscious pet parent · Format: Green-Screen Video
    05 How to film

    Filming & setup

    • Talking head to camera throughout. Green-screen build — farm footage and product footage are added in post.
    • Green-screen b-roll to capture separately: senior dog hesitating at the back door, a shortened walk, you watching from the kitchen, a bag being turned over.
    • Do not hold the product. Product footage is added in post.
    • Setup: vertical (9:16), even lighting on your face, clean background for keying, no strong shadows.
    06 Non-negotiables

    Brand rules

    • Talking head only — no product in hand at any point.
    • No medical claims. Never say cure, treat, heal, or fix. Say “supports a normal inflammatory response.”
    • Do not name competitor brands or show their labels legibly.
    • No study-count card on screen. The 40 years / 300+ studies reference stays spoken only for this concept.
    • Farm and working-animal footage is sourced by Ad Pros — you don't need to film any of it.
    • Pricing and guarantee stay exact: 53¢ a day · 60-day keep-the-canister guarantee.
