How these benchmarks were built: linear regression of each Meta metric against daily business-wide ncCPA over 90 days. The benchmark is the metric value at which the regression predicts business-wide ncCPA = $60. The four metrics need different improvements (~9-10% CPA reduction, +12-15% ROAS lift) because each lens correlates with the business outcome a bit differently. 7-Day Click First Conversion CPA has the tightest fit (R² = 0.82) — most predictive single in-platform metric. Higher R² = more reliable signal.
Why the First Click number looks so much higher

Only about 1 in 4 new customers (23%) first discovered Nurse Babes through a paid ad. The other 77% first heard about the brand somewhere else — a friend, an email, an organic search, direct traffic — and only came back through Meta later to actually buy.

So when First Click attribution asks “who introduced this customer?” — Meta gets credit for fewer of those first hellos. That’s why the same Meta spend looks roughly 2× more expensive under First Click vs Last Click. It’s not that Meta suddenly became inefficient — it’s that First Click is measuring something different.

How to use these three numbers

One is what we manage to. Two tell us what’s going on underneath.

Each benchmark answers a different question. Used together, they give a clear picture of Meta’s real contribution to the business.

The day-to-day number — In-Platform Meta ($59.99 CPA / 2.21x ROAS)

This is the number the Growth Marketer watches every day inside Meta Ads Manager. If in-platform CPA drifts above $59.99 (or ROAS drops below 2.21x), that’s the first signal something inside the Meta account needs attention — creative, audience, bid strategy, or testing cadence.

The closing-credit check — TW Last Click ($139.51 CPA)

A weekly cross-channel view. Different from in-platform because it asks: when Meta gets credit head-to-head against every other channel, how much is it actually closing? If in-platform looks good but TW Last Click is rough, it means Meta is taking credit in-platform for purchases another channel actually closed.

The discovery check — TW First Click ($242.38 CPA)

A monthly check on whether Meta is actually bringing new people into the brand vs. mostly closing customers who already knew about Nurse Babes. If Meta hits in-platform and Last Click but First Click stays rough, that’s a conversation about top-of-funnel — not an automatic spend reallocation.

These benchmarks are math, not a verdict. They tell us what Meta would need to deliver under each lens to hit $60 — they don’t account for halo effects between channels, brand-search lift, or the work Meta does that other tools end up taking credit for. We use them as guardrails, not as a single source of truth.

✓ Re-benchmark monthly · adjust if spend scale or organic mix shifts materially
