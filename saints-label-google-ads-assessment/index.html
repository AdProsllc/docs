04 Demand Gen

Demand Gen

Core Issue
Delivery
22 active Demand Gen campaigns. 18 of them are below Google's 50-conversion learning threshold. $13.9K of monthly Demand Gen spend is running in campaigns the algorithm cannot optimize. The May ncROAS drop from 1.92x to 1.47x is the structural problem showing up in the numbers.
Analysis

Each new creative batch launches as a new campaign instead of as an ad inside a consolidated campaign. At $50 to $300 daily budgets across 47 lifetime campaigns, conversion volume per campaign sits between 0 and 3 per day. That is below the threshold Maximize Conversions needs to learn cleanly. Every new batch starts the model from zero.

21 of the 22 active Demand Gen campaigns run on Maximize Conversions, not value-based bidding. The account optimizes for transaction count, ignoring revenue, despite a ROAS-targeted business goal.

When monthly Demand Gen spend ramped from $7.9K in March to $52K in May, ncROAS dropped from 1.92x to 1.47x. The model gets weaker signal per dollar as budget spreads thinner across more campaigns sitting below the learning threshold.

Recommendations
  • Consolidate 22 campaigns into roughly 4, grouped by creative theme. Future creative batches launch as ads inside those campaigns, not as new campaigns.
  • Move the consolidated campaigns to Maximize Conversion Value with a tROAS target tied to product margin.
  • Reallocate freed budget toward Brand Search and Performance Max while Demand Gen restructures.

05 Performance Max

Performance Max

Core Issue
Coverage + Delivery
Performance Max ncROAS climbed from 0.28x to 0.93x to 2.12x across March, April, and May as the campaign learned. 64% of Performance Max spend is delivering on Google Search, returning $7,423 of attributed value at $4,584 of cost. The campaign is improving and is being held back by roughly 63 changes in the last 30 days.
Analysis

The search-terms report confirms Performance Max is capturing real non-branded category demand: "bible study guide" (414 clicks, 35 conversions, 8.45% conversion rate), "bible study guides", "66 page bible study guide", "bible study guides book". The branded queries that do appear (saintslabel.com, faith sprouts the 66 roots journey) are correctly excluded by the negative list. Branded delivery is not happening on this campaign.

Performance Max is functioning as the de facto non-branded Search engine. It has to do that job without a dedicated Search campaign feeding it Quality Score signal, controlling intent tiers, or absorbing exact-match traffic.

The campaign also has roughly 63 changes logged in 30 days, including repeated tROAS step-downs visible in the campaign name (2.8x then 2.5x then 2.7x). Value-based bidding strategies need around 14 days of stable signal to optimize. Every target change resets that learning.

Recommendations
  • Lock the tROAS target. Pick one number based on product margin and hold it for a full 14-day cycle before reviewing.
  • Lift Performance Max budget as the learning trajectory continues. ncROAS doubled from April to May, and the campaign has not yet stabilized.
  • Treat Performance Max as the Shopping and YouTube engine. Let a restructured Non-Branded Search campaign capture exact-match Search intent.

06 Brand Search

Brand Search

Core Issue
Competition
Brand Search earned 3.60x ncROAS across the 90 days at $4,091 of spend. Monthly ncROAS held between 3.36x and 4.62x even as spend shrank to $362 in May. The channel is the most efficient earner in the Google account on every monthly cut, and it is being starved.
Analysis

Brand Search is at 70% impression share on the brand's own name, losing 30% to Ad Rank. On a brand with no obvious paid competitor on the Search Engine Results Page, that usually points to Quality Score components (ad relevance, expected click-through rate, landing page experience) rather than bid pressure. CPC at $0.98 is high for a brand with that competitive picture.

The campaign also shows repeated tROAS step-downs in the change history (8x to 7.5x to 6x). Like Performance Max, the campaign that is actually earning the most attributed credit per dollar is being tinkered with too aggressively to learn.

Brand Search spend dropped from $1,794 in March to $362 in May. The channel did not lose efficiency. It lost budget.

Recommendations
  • Audit Quality Score components on branded ad groups. Tighten headline-to-keyword alignment, test brand-specific responsive search ad variations.
  • Hold the tROAS target for a full 14-day cycle before changing it again.
  • Increase Brand Search budget until impression share clears 90%. The channel has produced 3.36x to 4.62x monthly ncROAS at every spend level this window.
  • Add Branded Shopping back at scale to defend the Search Engine Results Page from third-party listings appearing on the brand name.
07 Non-Branded Search

Non-Branded Search

Core Issue
Coverage + Delivery
A Non-Branded Search campaign was launched and returned 0.26x ncROAS on $1,739 of spend. The demand exists. Performance Max captures the same queries at 8.45% conversion rate. The relaunch needs structure the original campaign did not have.
Analysis

The Performance Max search-terms report proves the demand is there and converts well. "Bible study guide" runs at an 8.45% conversion rate at a $24.72 CPA inside Performance Max. A dedicated Search campaign on that exact query and its close variants should earn meaningful attributed credit.

The launched Non-Branded Search campaign did not perform because the structure could not isolate the intent. The keyword set was narrow, the budget was small ($1,739 across two months with most spent before any learning could occur), and the landing experience pulled high-intent searchers into the homepage rather than a product-detail page.

This is also the campaign that creates the Search foundation Performance Max needs. Without it, Performance Max has to do all the Search work using shopping signal and asset groups, which limits how much spend the account can put behind that intent profitably.

Recommendations
  • Relaunch Non-Branded Search with intent-tiered ad groups: category queries ("bible study guide"), specific queries ("66 page bible study guide"), feature queries ("bible study guide for beginners").
  • Start phrase match, layer exact match in once data builds. Use the converting Performance Max search terms as the seed keyword list.
  • Run the new campaign to a product-detail page landing experience, not the homepage. Measure landing page conversion rate separately from campaign ncROAS.
  • Build a shared negative keyword list so Non-Branded Search and Performance Max do not bid against each other on the same exact-match queries.

08 Shopping

Shopping

Core Issue
Coverage
Non-Brand Shopping earned 0.58x ncROAS on $2,344 of 90-day spend. The previous team scaled it down to $142 in the last 30 days. Performance Max is doing the Shopping work in its place, so the question is whether to invest in standalone Shopping at all.
Analysis

Search Impression Share on Non-Brand Shopping sits at 17.7%, with 55% of auctions lost to Ad Rank and 17.7% lost to Budget. The campaign is starved and structurally limited. Performance Max's product-data ads inside Google Search ($3,548 cost, $5,760 value) are proof that Shopping inventory can convert profitably for this account.

A standalone Non-Brand Shopping campaign would only make sense if it could outperform Performance Max's product-data delivery on the same query set. With Performance Max already capturing the converting Shopping intent, the case for re-investing in standalone Shopping is weaker than it looked from the in-platform view alone.

Recommendations
  • Hold Shopping at minimal spend for now. Use the budget to fund Non-Branded Search and Performance Max instead.
  • Audit the product feed for title, description, and image quality. Reviews on the product detail page mention "shipped from China, no author or production name", which is a Quality Score and trust signal risk that would affect any Shopping investment.
  • Revisit standalone Shopping after Non-Branded Search and Performance Max are stable. If demand exceeds what Performance Max can absorb, a properly structured Shopping campaign becomes the next expansion.
09 Tracking & Account Controls

Tracking & Account Controls

Core Issue
Delivery
Conversion tracking and account controls are set up correctly. In-platform Google Ads, TripleWhale Linear All, and TripleWhale Triple Attribution all reconcile to within reasonable variance, which is what a clean setup looks like.
Set up correctly
  • Single Primary purchase event from the Shopify Google Ads app. No double counting.
  • Data-driven attribution active on the Purchase event.
  • Enhanced Conversions enabled through Google Tag.
  • Dynamic conversion values flowing through correctly. In-platform AOV reconciles cleanly to TripleWhale.
  • Auto-apply recommendations fully off. 0 of 21 recommendations selected.
  • 90-day click window, 1-day view-through. Appropriate for this average order value and category.
Where over-management is undermining it
  • Roughly 63 changes on Performance Max and 57 on Brand Search in the last 30 days. Both run value-based bidding. Both are being prevented from finishing a learning cycle.
  • 21 of 22 Demand Gen campaigns run on Maximize Conversions. Conversion value is being ignored despite a value-based business target.
  • Add to Cart goal is flagged "Misconfigured" in the conversions UI. Minor, but worth fixing.
  • "Upgrade your conversion tracking" recommendation is currently surfaced. Worth confirming Google sees Data-Driven Attribution as fully applied.
The conversion tracking foundation is solid. The fix is letting the bidding strategies finish their learning cycles and aligning Demand Gen to value-based bidding. Not rebuilding tracking from scratch.
10 Primary Constraint

The single biggest thing limiting Google scale.

Demand Gen breaks under scale
while better channels are starved

Demand Gen carries 86.5% of Google spend and held 1.92x ncROAS through March and April. As it scaled to $52K in May, ncROAS dropped to 1.47x. The structural fragmentation across 22 active campaigns is the cause. Meanwhile Brand Search lost budget despite holding 3.36x to 4.62x monthly ncROAS, and Performance Max climbed to 2.12x while being throttled by over-management.

What proves it
  • Demand Gen monthly ncROAS: 1.92x → 1.92x → 1.47x as spend went $7.9K → $38K → $52K
  • Brand Search monthly ncROAS: 3.69x → 3.36x → 3.58x → 4.62x with spend dropping to $362 in May
  • Performance Max trajectory: 0.28x → 0.93x → 2.12x as the campaign learned
  • 18 of 22 active Demand Gen campaigns sit below Google's 50-conversion learning threshold
What the fix looks like

Restructure Demand Gen so it can absorb its existing budget without fragmenting signal. Move the freed efficiency toward Brand Search and Performance Max, both of which have headroom for more spend at their current ncROAS. Relaunch Non-Branded Search to give Performance Max the foundation it needs. These three changes compound: each one makes the others work better.

11 Path Forward

The First 30 Days

What we would execute, in what order, and what we would leave alone. Designed to recover wasted spend and reallocate toward the channels showing the strongest trajectories.

Week 1
  • Lock the tROAS target on Performance Max and Brand Search. End the repeated step-downs and let the bidders complete a 14-day learning cycle.
  • Pause the worst-performing signal-starved Demand Gen campaigns. Free up roughly $13K of monthly spend.
  • Shift initial freed budget into Brand Search until impression share moves above 90%, and into Performance Max while it is locked.
  • Audit Quality Score components on branded ad groups while the bidder learns.
Weeks 2 to 4
  • Consolidate 22 Demand Gen campaigns into roughly 4, grouped by creative theme. Future creative batches launch as ads inside those campaigns.
  • Move consolidated Demand Gen to Maximize Conversion Value with a margin-tied tROAS target.
  • Relaunch Non-Branded Search with intent-tiered ad groups and product-detail page landing destinations. Seed keywords from the Performance Max converting search terms.
  • Build a shared negative keyword list so Non-Branded Search and Performance Max do not bid against each other on the same exact-match queries.
Leave Alone
  • Conversion tracking. Data-Driven Attribution, Enhanced Conversions, single Primary purchase event. Keep all of it.
  • Auto-apply off. Do not change that.
  • Demand Gen as a channel. We restructure it. We do not kill it. The 1.92x ncROAS it held through March and April is real.
  • Brand Search campaign structure. We tune Quality Score and lift budget. We do not rebuild.
  • Performance Max brand exclusions. They are working.
12 For the Call

What we still want to learn

Three questions to answer before locking the budget reallocation.

  1. What is the contribution margin on the product?
    The right tROAS target for Performance Max, Brand Search, and the relaunched Non-Branded Search depends on contribution margin. Without it, every bid-strategy recommendation is a directional guess.
  2. Why was Non-Branded Search paused?
    The previous attempt returned 0.26x ncROAS. Knowing whether the team saw structural reasons or pulled the plug on the headline ncROAS number changes how we communicate the relaunch.
  3. What's the ceiling on Google spend you would be comfortable with?
    Brand Search and Performance Max have headroom for more budget at their current ncROAS. Knowing the comfort zone shapes how aggressively we sequence the rebuild.
In summary The work is restructuring Demand Gen so it stops breaking under scale, reallocating spend toward Brand Search and Performance Max while they have headroom, and relaunching Non-Branded Search with the structure the first attempt did not have.
