Unique Selling Proposition & Mechanism

The Core Insight

PF stops being an inflammation condition after ~4 weeks and becomes a degeneration condition. Traditional treatments (ice, NSAIDs, cortisone, stretching) target inflammation — which isn't the real problem. This is Koprez's wedge.

The Degenerative Spiral: Damaged tissue → reduced blood flow → no healing materials → tissue stays weak → tears more → even less blood flow. The cycle compounds until tissue is barely functional.

The Two-Part Solution

Part 1: Polyaxial Support Straps

Acts as a "second set of ligaments" — lifts the arch and offloads mechanical pressure crushing the fascia. Stops constant micro-tearing with every step. Like cables on the Golden Gate Bridge carrying the load.

Part 2: Biolateral Circular Compression

Multi-directional compression actively drives oxygen-rich blood into the starved tissue. Blood brings healing materials. Repair begins. Like a dry sponge dropped in water — drives nutrients into blood-starved tissue.

The Healing Snowball: Less tearing → more blood flow → more blood flow → less tearing. A self-reinforcing recovery loop.
