03 How to say it

The pitch, in two sentences

On a call $7,500 a month for creative, credited against a 5% performance fee. Once you pass $150,000 in monthly spend on our creative, the rate steps down — 4% past $500k, 3% past $1M, 2% past $2M.

Why $150,000

$7,500 is exactly 5% of $150,000 — the same rate as the first paid band. The retainer covers the fee up to that point and the curve runs straight through the threshold with no jump and no kink.

Why not a flat rate per tier

Applying one rate to all spend puts a $7,500 cliff at the threshold and makes the bill fall at $500k, $1M and $2M — the client pays less for spending more. Graduated bands remove both.

The effective rate floors around 2.7% at $5M, because the early bands keep their weight. To go lower at the top, the $2M+ band has to drop below 2%.
