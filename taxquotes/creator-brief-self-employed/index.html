03 Your role

Who we're looking for

You're self-employed (30-55), a freelancer, contractor, gig worker, or small business owner who fell behind on taxes. You had good income coming in, but nobody was withholding anything for you, and a year (or a few) slipped by unfiled. Now you owe, or you've got returns you never filed.

  • The feeling: you're great at your actual work, but the tax side buried you, and the "I'll deal with it next quarter" just kept snowballing.
  • The energy: real and relatable, a capable person owning up to the one thing they let slide.
04 The angle

Your concept

A first-person, self-employed testimonial. You admit that working for yourself meant no taxes were set aside, you fell behind (unfiled returns, a growing balance), and TaxQuotes' online tax investigation helped you see exactly where you stood, no judgment, no false promises. It ends on getting back in control. This angle works because no competitor is talking to the self-employed.

05 How to film

Filming & setup

  • Direct-to-camera talking head, real and unpolished. Filming in your actual work setting (workshop, van, desk, studio, chair) makes it land even harder.
  • Optional: show the 100% online process on a laptop or phone, and/or an unopened IRS letter.
  • Vertical 9:16, 15–60s (30s sweet spot). Clear audio, captions preferred.
06 Non-negotiables

Brand rules & compliance

  • Never promise a specific result. No guaranteed dollar reductions, no "eliminate your debt," no "settle for pennies on the dollar." This is a hard rule.
  • Use "see what you qualify for" / "based on your situation" framing, never guarantees.
  • Don't imply TaxQuotes is affiliated with, or endorsed by, the IRS or any government agency.
  • If you mention a real outcome, it must be true and can't be presented as the typical result.
  • Keep it honest, calm and approachable. This is someone's real financial stress, treat it with care, not hype.
