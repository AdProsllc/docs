The unit is the concept; the credit is the currency

A concept bundles its cuts

Each concept is one creative idea, delivered as a set of variations (cuts), and priced $100–400 by value. One credit = $1 of that value, so your package is simply a monthly credit balance: $10,000 = 10,000 credits. An AI image draws 100 credits, an AI video 200.

Volume sizes the plan

Your spend level sets how many concepts you need to test each month. The Testing Engine sizes that for you, and the Plan Builder assembles a credit package to produce them. The Strategy fee auto-tiers with that volume.

What you pay, simply You pay whichever is larger — the base fees (Strategy + Production) or the 5% performance fee on the spend behind Ad Pros creatives. Never both stacked. Media buying is billed separately.

How the model works

Two base fees that scale with volume, plus the performance upside.

1

Strategy Fee — auto-tiered

$2,500/mo at ≤25 concepts; $5,000/mo above 25 concepts. Covers creative strategy and the intelligence that drives it.

2

Production — by the concept, scales with volume

Each concept is a priced unit ($100–400) with its cuts bundled in. The production fee scales with how many concepts your spend level needs, so what you pay tracks the volume you actually run.

3

Performance — the engine

5% of the spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped. Absorbed: you pay whichever is larger — the base fees or the 5% — never both. Media buying is billed separately.

Sizing concepts

Concepts/mo = total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4 weeks. The strategy tier and production fee both follow that number. Model it on the Testing Engine tab.

Performance: absorbed

You pay max(base fees, 5% of AP spend). Below the cross-over you pay the base; above it, the 5% takes over and the base is absorbed into it — never both stacked.
