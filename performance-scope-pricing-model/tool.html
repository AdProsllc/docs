How to charge — the rules

  1. Match the variable fee to control. Outcome gate only when you own the funnel; royalty when you own the creative; % of spend when you only buy. Never gate a partial engagement on a number someone else moves.
  2. Price a la carte above the bundled slice. A standalone discipline should cost more than its share of the full-system price — you lose integration economies and the cross-discipline upside. That keeps the full system the best value.
  3. Never carry the downside gate on a partial scope. The 30%-over → 0% mechanic is only fair when you control every lever; on one discipline a miss is often the other party's fault.
  4. Use partial scopes as wedges. Media-only or creative-only can land the account and prove value — then expand into the full system, where your margin, alignment, and defensibility are highest.
  5. Lean into creative, be wary of media-only. Creative is your moat and prices rich on a clean royalty; media buying is platform-commoditized, so only take it standalone at a premium or as a path to more.

Notes. Full-system performance = (spend × target ROAS) × rate × gate. Creative fee = creative rate × spend × (% on our creatives), default 5% × 70%. Media = media rate × spend above the first $100k (the first $100k is unbilled, matching your tiered card). Retainers shown ($12K / $8K / $6K) are illustrative cost-to-serve floors — calibrate to your real costs and set a-la-carte retainers above their bundled share.
