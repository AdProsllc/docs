§Preamble

This Creator Content & Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Nurse Babes, a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] with offices at [ADDRESS] ("Brand"); and

[CREATOR LEGAL NAME], an individual residing at [CREATOR ADDRESS], professionally known as [CREATOR HANDLE / STAGE NAME] ("Creator").

Brand and Creator are each a "Party" and together the "Parties." This Agreement sets forth the terms under which Creator will produce content and provide services for Brand.

1.Definitions

"Content" means all photographs, videos, audio recordings, raw footage, b-roll, scripts, captions, edits, and other creative materials produced by Creator under this Agreement, whether delivered to Brand or retained by Creator.

"Deliverables" means the specific Content items Creator is required to produce per Section 3, including all finished video pieces, hook variants, and supporting raw footage.

"Paid Media" means any advertising placement that involves payment to a platform or distribution channel, including (without limitation) Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram, Audience Network, Messenger, Threads), TikTok Ads, Google Ads, YouTube Ads, programmatic display, and email/SMS marketing.

"Whitelisting" means Brand's authorization to run Paid Media advertising through Creator's social media handle(s), including via Meta Partnership Ads, TikTok Spark Ads, and equivalent platform mechanisms.

"Attributed Sales" means sales of Brand's products tracked through Trybe, Social Snowball, or successor platforms as being driven by Creator's Content or Creator's personal affiliate code.

"Tier" means the creator classification assigned to Creator: Tier 1 Proven, Tier 2 Active, Tier 3 Test, or Tier 4 Ambassador, per the Nurse Babes Creator Plan v1.

"Term" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.

2.Tier Classification & Compensation

2.1 Tier Assignment

Creator is engaged at [TIER 1 PROVEN / TIER 2 ACTIVE / TIER 3 TEST / TIER 4 AMBASSADOR] level. Compensation in this Section reflects the assigned Tier. Brand may promote or demote Creator's Tier at the end of any contract period based on performance, with corresponding compensation adjustments.

2.2 Base Compensation

Brand shall pay Creator a base retainer of [$300 / $500 / N/A] per calendar month during the Term, payable on or before the first business day of each month.

TierBase Retainer

2.3 Performance Revenue Share

For Tier 1 creators only, Brand shall pay Creator [10% / 12% (Premium)] of Attributed Sales tracked through Trybe-attribution for each Deliverable, for a period of twelve (12) months from each Deliverable's first launch date. Tier 2 and 3 creators are not eligible for revenue share. Tier 4 ambassadors are compensated solely through the Affiliate Commission in Section 2.5.

2.4 Winner Bonus

For Tier 1 creators, Brand shall pay Creator a one-time bonus of $500 per Deliverable that achieves "Winner" status, defined as: (a) cumulative Brand ad spend on that Deliverable exceeding $10,000, AND (b) return on ad spend equal to or greater than Brand's account-level ROAS for the trailing 30-day period. Winner Bonus is paid within 30 days of confirmation.

2.5 Affiliate Commission

Creator will receive a personal affiliate code via Social Snowball. Brand shall pay Creator a commission of [10% (Tier 1-3) / 15% (Tier 4 Ambassador)] on all Attributed Sales tracked through Creator's affiliate code, paid monthly via Social Snowball within the standard payout window.

2.6 Product Allocation

Brand shall provide Creator with sample product as required for content production, up to a maximum of [2 pairs per quarter] for Tier 1-2 creators. Tier 3 Test creators receive one (1) sample pair at engagement and become eligible for additional product upon advancement to Tier 2.

2.7 Payment Method & Currency

All payments shall be made in U.S. Dollars via Trybe (for base + revenue share + winner bonus) and Social Snowball (for affiliate commission). Payments held by either platform are subject to platform terms and processing windows.

3.Scope of Work & Deliverables

3.1 Monthly Deliverables (Tier 1 SKU B)

  • Ten (10) full video Deliverables per calendar month, each between 15 and 60 seconds in length, formatted 9:16 vertical for use across Meta Reels, TikTok, Instagram Stories, and equivalent placements.
  • Two (2) fresh hook variants per calendar month, each approximately 5 seconds in length, designed for A/B testing against existing top-performing creative.
  • Raw footage and b-roll from each shoot, delivered alongside finished pieces.

3.1.alt Monthly Deliverables (Tier 2 SKU A)

Fifteen (15) full video Deliverables per calendar month, same specifications as 3.1.

3.1.alt Monthly Deliverables (Tier 3 SKU T)

One (1) to two (2) Deliverables over the 30-day initial engagement period. Advancement to Tier 2 is contingent on Brand approval of submitted Deliverables meeting brand voice and quality standards.

3.2 Content Briefs

Brand shall deliver written content briefs to Creator via Trybe at least seven (7) days before each Deliverable due date. Briefs will specify: subject, intended hook, key messaging points, format, and any required visual or copy elements. Creator agrees to follow brief guidance unless Brand expressly grants creative latitude.

3.4 Quality Standards

  • Resolution: minimum 1080p (Full HD) for all video Deliverables
  • Audio: clean and intelligible voice recording where script is present
  • Format: MP4 or MOV, H.264 codec, native vertical 9:16 aspect ratio (1080×1920)
  • FTC #ad / #sponsored disclosure visible within first 3 seconds of every paid Deliverable
  • Brand product (Ortho Pro 1.0 or current featured SKU) clearly visible in finished piece

3.5 Compliance Standards

All Content produced under this Agreement must comply with the Nurse Babes Brand Voice Guardrails (Schedule A). Failure to meet compliance standards is grounds for rejection of submission, revision without compensation, and counts toward Creator's strike record.

4.Grant of Rights

This is the most important section of this Agreement. Brand requires perpetual paid-media rights on all Content delivered under this Agreement. This is non-negotiable. Brand has previously lost significant revenue due to expiring usage rights and will not enter into agreements that restrict long-term use of Content.

4.1 Perpetual Paid-Media Usage Rights

Creator grants Brand a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable license to use, reproduce, distribute, modify, edit, derivative-work, publicly display, and publicly perform all Content (including all delivered Deliverables AND all raw footage, b-roll, and unselected material) for Brand's Paid Media advertising, marketing, and promotional purposes, in any and all media now known or later developed. This license survives termination or expiration of this Agreement.

4.2 Whitelisting Authorization

Creator authorizes Brand to: (a) run Meta Partnership Ads through Creator's Facebook and Instagram accounts; (b) run TikTok Spark Ads through Creator's TikTok account; (c) run equivalent whitelisted ads on YouTube, Pinterest, Snapchat, and any other platform Brand may use. Creator shall provide Brand with platform-specific authorization codes / Spark codes / Partnership Ads access within seven (7) days of Brand's request. This whitelisting authorization remains in effect during the Term and for any subsequent period during which Brand continues to run signed-asset Paid Media.

4.3 Name and Likeness

Creator grants Brand the right to use Creator's name, social media handle, image, likeness, and biographical information in connection with the Content and the promotion of Brand. This right is exclusive to Brand within the Restricted Category (Section 5.1) during the Term, and non-exclusive in all other contexts.

4.4 Derivative Works

Brand may edit, recut, remix, repurpose, translate, and otherwise create derivative works from any Content, including combining Creator's Content with content from other creators or Brand-produced material. Creator shall not be entitled to additional compensation for derivative works.

4.5 Reservation of Rights

Creator retains ownership of underlying Content for Creator's personal use, including: (a) posting on Creator's own organic social channels; (b) including in Creator's professional portfolio, sizzle reel, or case study materials; (c) using in Creator's own promotional materials for Creator's services. Creator may not sublicense Content to other brands during the Term and the Restricted Period (Section 5.1).

4.6 Moral Rights Waiver

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Creator waives any "moral rights" or similar rights in the Content with respect to Brand's permitted uses under this Agreement.

5.Exclusivity & Restrictions

5.1 Category Exclusivity

During the Term and for thirty (30) days following expiration or termination (the "Restricted Period"), Creator shall not create, post, or accept compensation for content promoting any product or service in the Restricted Category, defined as: nurse-specific footwear, healthcare-worker footwear, orthopedic / comfort footwear marketed to professionals on their feet, including without limitation: Clove, Kuru Footwear, Snibbs, Nurse Mates, Crocs Bistro, Comfortorthowear, Hike Footwear, Dansko, Vionic, Hoka (as it pertains to healthcare positioning), and any successor or equivalent brand.

5.2 First-Look Right

For twelve (12) months following expiration or termination of this Agreement, Creator shall notify Brand in writing of any proposed engagement with a competitor in the Restricted Category before accepting such engagement. Brand shall have seven (7) days from notice to match or exceed the competitor's offer. If Brand declines to match, Creator may proceed with the competing engagement free of further restriction.

5.3 Permitted Activities

Nothing in this Section restricts Creator from: (a) accepting engagements in adjacent but non-competing categories including apparel, scrubs, wellness, fitness, accessories, food, beauty, or general lifestyle; (b) maintaining Creator's own service business (e.g. piercing studio, salon, retail); (c) creating organic content unrelated to the Restricted Category.

5.4 Disclosure Compliance

Creator shall comply with all applicable Federal Trade Commission ("FTC") endorsement guidelines and all platform-specific disclosure requirements. Creator shall include #ad or #sponsored disclosure visibly within the first three (3) seconds of any video Deliverable, and shall not represent personal opinion as independent review where compensation has been provided.

6.Brand Voice & Compliance Guardrails

Creator agrees to adhere to the Nurse Babes Brand Voice Guardrails (Schedule A, attached and incorporated). Schedule A includes:

  • List of restricted phrases that may not appear in Creator's voice (health/medical claims)
  • List of preferred alternatives
  • FTC disclosure requirements
  • Platform-specific advertising policies (Meta health/wellness restrictions)
  • Exempt content: customer testimonial quotes (third-party voice)

Violation of Schedule A may result in rejection of Deliverable, revision without additional compensation, and counts toward Creator's strike record (Section 9.4).

7.Confidentiality

7.1 Creator acknowledges that during the Term, Brand may share Confidential Information, including: business strategy, financial information, creative briefs, performance data, competitor analysis, customer data, product roadmap, marketing plans, and the terms of this Agreement.

7.2 Creator shall: (a) hold Confidential Information in confidence; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without Brand's prior written consent; (c) use Confidential Information solely for purposes of performing under this Agreement; (d) take reasonable measures to protect Confidential Information.

7.3 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.

7.4 Confidential Information does not include information that is: (a) publicly available through no fault of Creator; (b) known to Creator prior to disclosure; (c) independently developed; or (d) required to be disclosed by law (subject to advance notice to Brand).

8.Representations & Warranties

8.1 Creator Representations. Creator represents and warrants that:

  1. Creator has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to grant the rights granted herein;
  2. Creator is not subject to any conflicting obligation that would prevent performance under this Agreement;
  3. All Content produced is original to Creator or properly licensed, and does not infringe any third-party intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights;
  4. All claims, statements, and representations made by Creator in the Content are truthful, accurate, and substantiated;
  5. Creator's social media accounts comply with each platform's terms of service and are in good standing;
  6. Creator will comply with all applicable laws, including FTC endorsement guidelines and consumer protection laws.

8.2 Brand Representations. Brand represents and warrants that:

  1. Brand has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement;
  2. Brand's products do not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights;
  3. Brand will provide Creator with accurate product information for use in Content.

9.Term, Termination & Renewal

9.1 Initial Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of [THREE (3) / SIX (6) / TWELVE (12)] months (the "Initial Term"). Tier 1-2 standard is three (3) months. Tier 4 Ambassador standard is twelve (12) months.

9.2 Auto-Renewal

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive three (3)-month periods (each, a "Renewal Term"), provided that Creator's performance during the trailing thirty (30)-day period prior to renewal meets all of the following thresholds:

  • Cost per acquisition (CPA) on Creator's Content less than or equal to Brand's blended account CPA;
  • Return on ad spend (ROAS) on Creator's Content greater than or equal to 2.0;
  • Submission cadence: minimum 80% of Deliverables submitted on or before SLA deadlines;
  • No outstanding compliance violations.

If any threshold is not met, this Agreement will not auto-renew. Brand may, at its discretion, offer a renewal at the same or different Tier on revised terms.

9.3 Tier Promotion / Demotion

Brand may promote Creator to a higher Tier (with corresponding compensation increase) upon meeting performance thresholds defined in the Nurse Babes Creator Plan v1. Brand may also demote Creator to a lower Tier if performance falls below threshold for two (2) consecutive Renewal Terms.

9.4 Strike-Based Termination

Creator may be terminated immediately for cause upon accumulation of three (3) "strikes" within any rolling 90-day period. A strike is recorded for: (a) declined Deliverable due to brand voice or compliance violation; (b) missed SLA deadline by more than 5 calendar days without prior notice; (c) violation of exclusivity or confidentiality.

9.5 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within ten (10) days of written notice; (b) becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors; (c) is convicted of a felony or engages in conduct that materially harms the reputation of the other Party.

9.6 Termination for Convenience

Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party. Creator shall complete any in-progress Deliverables. Brand shall pay Creator base compensation through the notice period, plus any earned revenue share or affiliate commission earned through the termination date.

9.7 Survival

Sections 4 (Grant of Rights), 5.1-5.2 (Restricted Category obligations during the Restricted Period), 7 (Confidentiality), 8 (Representations & Warranties), 10 (Indemnification), 11 (Limitation of Liability), 12 (Governing Law), and any earned but unpaid compensation obligations shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.

10.Indemnification

10.1 By Creator. Creator shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Brand and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to: (a) any breach by Creator of this Agreement; (b) any third-party claim that the Content infringes intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights; (c) any false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claim made by Creator in the Content; (d) any violation of applicable law by Creator, including FTC disclosure requirements.

10.2 By Brand. Brand shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Creator from and against any and all third-party claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising out of: (a) any breach by Brand of this Agreement; (b) any third-party claim that Brand's product itself (independent of Creator's portrayal) is defective or violates law; (c) Brand-supplied claims about Brand's products that Brand instructs Creator to make and that prove to be inaccurate.

11.Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR (A) THE INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS IN SECTION 10, (B) BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS IN SECTION 7, OR (C) WILLFUL MISCONDUCT OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE, NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL COMPENSATION PAID OR PAYABLE TO CREATOR UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY. IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR OTHERWISE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

12.General Provisions

12.1 Independent Contractor. Creator is engaged as an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer of Brand. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship. Creator is responsible for all taxes on compensation received hereunder.

12.2 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except that Brand may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

12.3 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [STATE], without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction in the state and federal courts located in [COUNTY, STATE].

12.4 Dispute Resolution. Before initiating litigation, the Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation for at least thirty (30) days. Unresolved disputes shall be settled by binding arbitration in [COUNTY, STATE] under the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

12.5 Notices. All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the addresses specified above (or to such updated address as the Party may designate in writing).

12.6 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with Schedule A and any other attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

12.7 Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties.

12.8 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

12.9 No Waiver. A Party's failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that provision or any other provision.

12.10 Counterparts & Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, and electronic signatures (including via DocuSign or HelloSign) are deemed equivalent to original signatures.

A.Schedule A — Brand Voice & Compliance Guardrails

A.1 Restricted Phrases (Creator may not use in Creator's own voice)

  • "Pain-free" / "pain free" / "eliminates pain"
  • "Treats" / "cures" / "heals" / "fixes" any condition
  • "Medical-grade" / "therapeutic" / "clinically proven"
  • "FDA approved" or any government-certification implication
  • "Plantar fasciitis cure" / "arthritis relief" / specific condition treatment claims
  • "Doctor recommended" / "podiatrist recommended" unless specifically authorized by Brand with a named, real medical advisor
  • "Trusted Solution for All Foot Conditions" or equivalent

A.2 Preferred Alternatives

  • "All-day comfort"
  • "Supportive" / "support"
  • "Designed for long shifts" / "Built for 12-hour days"
  • "Easy on your feet"
  • "Cloud-like comfort" / "Feels like nothing"
  • "Show up strong"
  • Personal experience language: "I wear these for [X hours]"; "These replaced my [Y]"; "After [Z], I switched"

A.3 Exempt Content (Customer Testimonial Quotes)

Creator may quote verbatim customer testimonials and Trustpilot reviews verbatim, including testimonials containing language otherwise restricted by Schedule A.1. The exemption applies only when (a) the source is clearly third-party, (b) the testimonial is quoted accurately and in context, and (c) the testimonial is attributed (name + role where possible).

A.4 FTC Disclosure Requirements

  • #ad or #sponsored hashtag visible within first 3 seconds of video Deliverables
  • On-image disclosure for static placements
  • Spoken disclosure for any voice-led content greater than 30 seconds
  • Disclosure language must be conspicuous — same prominence as primary message

A.5 Platform-Specific Compliance

  • Meta 2025 Health/Wellness Restrictions: Brand and Content must not be categorized as Health/Wellness in ad account or this triggers bottom-funnel tracking limits. All Content reviewed for compliance before submission.
  • TikTok Community Guidelines: no medical claims, no implied health treatments
  • FTC Gravity Defyer 2025 ruling: bright-line warning on pain-relief claims for footwear — all Creator-voice content must be compliant
