Push + Pull (forces FOR switching)

  • Push: failed mainstream products (retinols irritated, $100 serums underdelivered, fragrance flared eczema)
  • Push: health scare or pregnancy raised the bar on what's allowed on skin
  • Pull: 3-5 ingredient simplicity vs. 40-ingredient label fatigue
  • Pull: social proof — before/afters, influencer adoption

Anxiety + Habit (forces AGAINST switching)

  • Anxiety: "Will it smell like beef?" (the #1 category-wide objection)
  • Anxiety: "Will it feel greasy on my face?"
  • Anxiety: "Will it break me out?" (~20% of users report breakouts)
  • Habit: entrenched routines with name-brand serums and acids
Evil Goods already solves anxieties 1 + 2 in the product itself: "whipped" texture answers the greasiness objection, scented variants (Lavender / Citrus / Sandalwood) answer the smell objection. But the ads don't directly call out either anxiety — that's a missed creative angle.
