    Signal-Based Outbound Playbook

    20 signal campaigns for AdPros, ranked by priority and value

    Cold blasting isn't working. Break the frame.

    We're sending a lot of cold email and almost no one is replying. Scraping leads that match our ICP and blasting them cold isn't delivering, and it isn't going to. This playbook comes at outbound from a different angle: signals. Instead of who fits, we reach a brand the week something changed and lead with value, not a pitch. The goal is simple: find the angles that break through and actually put leads in front of us.

    01 / The shift

    Signal-first, not sender-first

    An active ad library says a brand could buy. A signal says they're buying now. Two upgrades to our motion deliver almost all the gain:

    A signal layer in front

    New sources that surface the trigger, not just the fit.

    A judgment layer in the middle

    An LLM reads every lead, scores it, and routes it to the right sequence.

    Value-first campaigns built on the signal run double-digit reply rates against a 3 to 5 percent cold baseline. The give is the lever, and BIE is the give.
    02 / Build the brain first

    Capturing signals is easy. Activating them is the work.

    One layer sits under all 20 campaigns. Build it before campaign two. It's a Claude Code / Catalina job on top of the existing cold-email pipeline.

    1

    Qualify

    An LLM reads each lead and returns keep or drop with a reason. The reasons sharpen the ICP every run.

    2

    Score

    Tier each lead 1, 2, or 3 on fit plus timing. Saying no is most of the job.

    3

    Dedupe

    One brand often fires several signals a week. Act once, not three times.

    4

    Route

    Tier 1 gets a manual BIE teardown and a Nehal Loom. Tier 2 and 3 go automated in Instantly.

    5

    Loop

    Pipe every outcome back so next month's list starts smarter. Measure pipeline by source, not meetings.

    04 / The 20, in detail

    The 20, in detail

    Click any campaign to expand its description plus the signal, the BIE offer, the tech, and the data we're not using yet.

    Wave 1 Launch now

    A brand whose ads have gone stale is losing performance and usually knows it. We arrive with the fix half-done.

    SignalRunning Meta ads, but the same creatives have been live 60+ days, or fewer than ~5 are active.
    OfferA teardown of the stale ads plus three fresh angles to test.
    DetectMeta Ad Library API (metapi, already running) reading creative age and count; Foreplay or Atria for depth.
    UntappedCreative age and active count. We check has / has-not a library and skip where fatigue shows.

    A brand that just started running Meta ads is spending without a system yet and hasn't committed to an agency. We catch them first.

    SignalFirst Meta ad appeared in the last 30 to 90 days.
    OfferA read on what breaks at their spend level, plus a starter testing plan.
    DetectA first-seen date filter on the existing Meta Ad Library pull.
    UntappedAdvertiser first-seen date. We can't tell a three-year advertiser from a three-week one.

    An open paid-media req means budget is approved and the work is piling up before the hire lands. We offer to carry it now.

    SignalAn open req for a media buyer, performance / growth marketer, creative strategist, UGC lead, or AI video creator.
    OfferA teardown of what that role struggles with in its first 90 days.
    DetectGreenhouse, Lever, Ashby boards (free, no auth), LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Apify scrapers.
    UntappedThe job-description text, not just the title. It names their pain in their words.

    Every lead we've ever emailed that didn't opt out is a warm, paid-for asset, not a dead list. We re-clean and re-enrich the whole history and run it as a fresh campaign at near-zero cost.

    SignalAny lead from a past Instantly campaign that never opted out, with openers and clickers prioritized.
    OfferA fresh teardown built on re-enriched data: current ads, current signals.
    DetectExport past Instantly campaigns, re-verify emails (Findymail / BetterContact or in-house), re-enrich via metapi plus firmographics through the qualification brain.
    UntappedOur entire historical send list. We treat finished campaigns as spent instead of a compounding owned asset.

    Wave 2 Next

    A brand running 20+ ads values creative and has budget to feed it. These are our best-fit, longest-retaining clients.

    Signal20+ active creatives.
    OfferA creative benchmark against their category leaders.
    DetectMeta Ad Library count; Foreplay or Magicbrief to rank by volume.
    UntappedCreative count as a fit score. High velocity means an ideal client.

    A brand moving from Meta-only into TikTok (or back) needs help managing a second channel without lighting money on fire.

    SignalA Meta-only brand that just started running TikTok ads, or the reverse.
    OfferA read on porting their winning Meta angles to the new channel.
    DetectCross-reference the Meta Ad Library against the TikTok Creative Center.
    UntappedChannel count. The second-channel start is the buying moment.

    Someone from a brand looked at adpros.com and didn't book. They're already shopping us; we just close the loop.

    SignalA brand that visited adpros.com but didn't book.
    Offer"Saw your team looking at us, here's a teardown so the trip was worth it."
    DetectRB2B on adpros.com for company-level de-anonymization.
    UntappedOur own site traffic. We don't know who's already shopping us.

    Brands scaling spend into peak season have the most to gain or lose and a short window to fix creative. Launch this in August.

    SignalSpend and creative count climbing into peak season.
    OfferA BFCM creative and offer audit before the window closes.
    DetectMeta Ad Library spend and creative trend, gated to seasonal timing.
    UntappedSpend trend over time. A point-in-time check misses the ramp.

    Wave 3 Build

    A role posted twice usually means the first hire fell through. That brand is in more pain than one that posted once. Run as a hot filter on top of campaign 3.

    SignalThe same paid req posted twice.
    Offer"Bridge the gap while you hire, here's the plan."
    DetectGreenhouse / Lever / Ashby plus LinkedIn, diffing repost dates.
    UntappedRepost detection. Invisible without tracking posting history.

    A new CMO or growth lead wants an early win and is open to new agencies in their first 90 days. We arrive with a plan for the brand they just inherited.

    SignalA CMO, VP Growth, or Head of Performance who joined a DTC brand in the last 90 days.
    OfferA 90-day paid plan for the brand they just inherited.
    DetectLinkedIn job-change feed via Trigify or Clay; Apollo for the title.
    UntappedExec tenure. The under-90-days window is the opening.

    A fresh batch of creatives means a launch is live and the brand is actively spending to make it work. Perfect moment for a teardown.

    SignalA fresh creative cluster in the ad library this month.
    OfferA launch-creative teardown with angles to scale the winner.
    DetectMeta Ad Library creative-cluster detection (a dated batch of new creatives).
    UntappedCreative launch clusters. We see ads exist, not that a batch just dropped.

    The direct competitors of brands we already win for are the easiest to win next. We lead with proof from their category.

    SignalDirect competitors of our closed-won brands.
    Offer"We run [category leader]. Here's what we'd do for you," plus a comparative teardown.
    DetectStoreleads or Charm.io category sets; Meta Ad Library competitive sets.
    UntappedA competitor graph anchored on our own client roster.

    Someone reacting to a competitor agency or ad influencer is thinking about the exact problem we solve. We pick up the thread they started.

    SignalLiked or commented on a competitor agency's or ad influencer's post.
    OfferA teardown that picks up the exact thread they engaged with.
    DetectGojiberry or Trigify; PhantomBuster or Apify on post engagers.
    UntappedThe engagement graph. We don't watch who reacts to our category.

    Wave 4 Compounding

    A brand that just dropped its agency is the hottest lead that exists and has a gap to fill this week. Rare, high-value, worth a manual touch.

    SignalDropped an agency's UTM or pixel, or a public "looking for a new agency" post.
    OfferA same-week BIE teardown plus a transition plan and a short Nehal Loom.
    DetectBuiltWith or Wappalyzer pixel changes; Reddit, X, and LinkedIn keyword monitors.
    UntappedTech-stack change over time and social complaint monitoring.

    A fresh round means new budget with a mandate to grow. We show where it compounds fastest.

    SignalJust raised seed or Series A.
    OfferA category growth benchmark and where the new budget compounds fastest.
    DetectCrunchbase or Tracxn; funding news feeds.
    UntappedFunding events. Not in our current pull at all.

    A marketer who got results with us at one brand lands at a new one. A warm intro disguised as a cold lead.

    SignalA marketer from a happy AdPros client just joined a new brand.
    Offer"We drove results for you at [prior brand], let's do it here."
    DetectLinkedIn job-change tracking mapped against the client roster.
    UntappedThe client-alumni graph. We don't track where past contacts go.

    Warm leads through the partner network carry the highest trust and lowest cost. The grown-up version maps account overlap with partners directly.

    SignalA warm lead surfaced through the partner / ecosystem network.
    OfferA co-branded teardown delivered through the referring partner.
    DetectA structured partner program plus CRM referral tracking; Crossbeam-style overlap.
    UntappedA partner-referral motion feeding outbound. It doesn't exist yet.

    Wave 5 Later

    A brand that just added Triple Whale or Northbeam is scaling paid and serious about measurement. We speak their language on day one.

    SignalJust added Triple Whale, Northbeam, or a new attribution / Klaviyo stack.
    OfferA read on what the new data will reveal and how to act on it.
    DetectBuiltWith, Wappalyzer, or HG Insights technographics.
    UntappedTechnographic change detection. Not wired in.

    A new store or a move to Shopify Plus signals a brand investing to scale, and in need of demand to fill it.

    SignalA new Shopify store, or a migration to Shopify Plus.
    OfferA launch ad strategy, or a scale-up audit for the replatform.
    DetectStoreleads or BuiltWith new-store and platform-tier detection.
    UntappedPlatform-tier and store-age data. Not in our pull.

    A brand whose reviews or traffic are climbing fast is outgrowing its current setup. We catch them on the way up.

    SignalFast-climbing review counts or a traffic spike.
    OfferA "you're scaling, here's where paid breaks next" teardown.
    DetectTrustpilot, Yotpo, or Okendo review velocity; SimilarWeb traffic trend.
    UntappedReview-count and traffic trendlines. We don't track momentum.

    07 / What we're not using yet

    The data sitting right there that we ignore

    Inside the ad data we already pull

    • Advertiser first-seen date — new vs established
    • Creative count, age, refresh cadence — fatigue and velocity
    • Creative format mix — static vs video vs UGC
    • The ad's offer, angle, and landing page
    • Estimated spend / impressions tier
    • Number of channels they run
    • Creative launch clusters

    Cheap enrichments and our own exhaust

    • Store revenue band + employee count — Storeleads / Charm

    • The LinkedIn engagement graph
    • Our first-party exhaust — the whole Instantly history, opens, clicks, site visits, client alumni
