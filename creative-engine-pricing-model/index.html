The three fees

How the model works

Two base fees that scale with volume, plus the performance upside.

1

Strategy Fee — auto-tiered

$2,500/mo at ≤25 concepts; $5,000/mo above 25 concepts. Covers creative strategy and the intelligence moat. Carries a ~40% delivery cost.

2

Production — by the concept, scales with volume

Each concept is a priced unit ($100–400) with its cuts bundled. The production fee scales with how many concepts the spend level needs, so cost and revenue track volume.

3

Performance — the engine

5% of the spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped. Absorbed: the client pays whichever is larger — the base fees or the 5%. Media buying is billed separately.

Sizing concepts

Concepts/mo = total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4 weeks. Strategy tier and production fee both follow that number.

Performance: absorbed

Revenue = max(base fees, 5% of AP spend). Below the cross-over the client pays the base; above it, the 5% takes over and the base is absorbed into it — never both stacked.

Guardrails & termsConcepts bundle their cuts · 90-day minimum · $500 internal onboarding cost · guarantee miss = refund concept credits, no cash, waive 5% (worst case = strategy fee only, with production COGS already sunk).

Concepts/month are sized from the spend level, then everything follows: the strategy fee auto-tiers (≤25 → $2,500, >25 → $5,000) and the production fee and its COGS scale with concept volume. Cost includes real creative-production COGS plus strategy delivery. Media buying and overhead/G&A are excluded — this is gross margin.

Reference

Assumptions

Defaults and definitions. Everything is priced per concept — the sellable unit, with its cuts bundled in. The two cost tabs reconcile exactly: the Concept Matrix is the Production & Credits per-cut cost multiplied by the concept’s variation count.

Units & definitions

Concept
The sellable unit. One creative idea, delivered as a set of variations (cuts). Priced $100–400 by value.
Variation (cut)
One finished, production-ready output within a concept. A 4-variation concept ships 4 cuts.
Loaded cost / cut
labor + token for a single cut — the figure on Production & Credits.
Cost / concept
loaded cost per cut × variations — the bridge to the Concept Matrix.
Cost / variation
Blended COGS ÷ total variations in a mix (a per-cut read on the calculator).
Credit
1 credit = $1 of concept list value. A package is a monthly credit balance; each concept draws credits equal to its price.

Pricing & fees

Strategy fee
Auto-tiers: $2,500/mo at ≤25 concepts, $5,000/mo above 25.
Concept price
Set by value, $100–400 (VSLs over 3 min exempt). Not a cost-up; cost-up (2×) is only a floor benchmark.
Performance fee
5% of spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped, absorbed (revenue = max of base fees vs 5%).
Media buying
Separate service — not priced in this model.

Cost basis

Editor labor
Editor hours × $14/hr (Medellín). Live-action rows include shoot & talent time.
Token / generation
$0.10/sec × 3 raw generations per production-ready cut (e.g. 240s b-roll = $72; 60s video = $18).
Production COGS (model)
concepts × (avg price ÷ markup), default markup 2×.
Creative strategy cost
40% of the strategy fee.
Overhead / G&A
Not modeled. These are gross-margin figures; net margin only arrives with performance scale.

Concepts / month sizing

Testing budget %
20% of total spend on creative testing.
CPA
Cost per new customer, default $100.
CPA test multiplier
2× default — spend per concept to read a valid test.
Concepts / month
total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4 weeks. Drives both the strategy tier and the production fee.
Terms90-day minimum · $500 internal onboarding cost · guarantee miss = refund concept credits, no cash, waive 5% (worst case = strategy fee only, with production COGS already sunk). Spend modeled $30k → $3M/mo.
