The three fees

How the model works

Each fee does a different job. The base two are floors; the third is the upside.

1

Strategy Fee — the base

$2,500/mo for up to 25 concepts (≤100 ads); $5,000/mo above 25 concepts. Covers strategy and the moat — taste, intelligence, and the system that turns volume into winners. Carries a ~40% delivery cost, so it runs at roughly a 60% margin.

2

Production Fee — credits at 2× cost

A monthly production fee buys credits priced at twice loaded production cost (labor + tokens). Near cost-recovery by design — production is not where the margin lives, so the entry stays competitive.

3

Performance — the engine

5% of the spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped. Aligned with the client’s success and our highest-margin line. This is where an account becomes genuinely profitable as it scales.

Production sizing

Concepts/mo = total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4. Variations = concepts × variations-per-concept. The Production Calculator then turns a chosen ad mix into credits, cost, and price.

Performance: two modes

From $0: 5% from the first dollar, fully additive.

With threshold: the 5% only adds once it clears a base — the production fee, or production + strategy. Below the threshold, the client pays just the base fees.

Guardrails & termsMinimum 4 variations per concept · credits roll 90 days · 90-day minimum commitment for ramp · $500 internal onboarding cost · guarantee miss = refund credits, no cash, waive 5% (worst case = strategy fee only).

Reference

Assumptions

The defaults and definitions behind every number in this model. Levers on the Financial Model tab can flex most of these; the values below are the starting points.

Pricing

Strategy fee
$2,500/mo for ≤25 concepts (≤100 ads); $5,000/mo above 25 concepts.
Production fee
Monthly fee for credits, default $5,000. Credits priced at the markup over loaded cost.
Production markup
2.0× floor on loaded production cost (labor + tokens).
Credit price
$1.00 per credit. Credits per asset = ceil(cost × markup ÷ credit price).
Performance fee
5% of spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped.

Cost basis

Creative strategy cost
40% of the strategy fee (delivery / strategist labor) — strategy runs at ~60% margin.
Production COGS
Production fee ÷ markup (e.g. at 2×, half the production fee).
Performance cost
Treated as ~0 — performance is effectively pure margin.
Asset cost basis
Refreshed token costs (cheaper models / reduced generation ratios). Labor held constant; only the token share flexes with the Token Price Index.

Production sizing calculator

Testing budget %
20% of total spend allocated to creative testing.
CPA
Cost to acquire a new customer, default $100 (typical range $50–$200).
CPA test multiplier
2× default (range 1–6×) — spend needed per concept to read a valid test.
Variations per concept
4 default (model minimum is 4).
Concepts / month
total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4 weeks.
