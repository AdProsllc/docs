The three fees

How the model works

Each fee does a different job. The base two are floors; the third is the upside.

1

Strategy Fee — the base

$2,500/mo for up to 25 concepts (≤100 ads); $5,000/mo above 25 concepts. Covers strategy and the moat — taste, intelligence, and the system that turns volume into winners. Carries a ~40% delivery cost (runs ~60% margin).

2

Production — sold by the concept

Each concept is a priced unit ($100–400) with its variations bundled in. The concept price is set by value, not token cost; cost per concept just sets the margin.

3

Performance — the engine

5% of the spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped. Aligned with the client’s success and our highest-margin line. This is where an account becomes genuinely profitable as it scales.

Sizing concepts

Concepts/mo = total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4 weeks. The Production Calculator then turns a chosen concept mix into cost, price and blended margin.

Performance: two modes

From $0: 5% from the first dollar, fully additive.

With threshold: the 5% only adds once it clears a base — the production fee, or production + strategy.

Guardrails & termsConcepts bundle their cuts · 90-day minimum commitment for ramp · $500 internal onboarding cost · guarantee miss = refund concept credits, no cash, waive 5% (worst case = strategy fee only).

Reference

Assumptions

The defaults and definitions behind the model. Everything is expressed per concept — the sellable unit, with its cuts bundled in.

Pricing

Strategy fee
$2,500/mo for ≤25 concepts (≤100 ads); $5,000/mo above 25 concepts.
Concept price
Set by value, $100–400 per concept (VSLs over 3 min exempt). Not derived from token cost.
Production markup
2.0× floor used for the per-cut cost-up reference and production-fee gross profit.
Performance fee
5% of spend behind Ad Pros creatives, uncapped.

Cost basis

Cost per concept
(labor + token) for one cut × the cuts bundled into that concept. Shown directly on the Concept Matrix.
Creative strategy cost
40% of the strategy fee (delivery / strategist labor).
Production COGS
Production fee ÷ markup (e.g. half the fee at 2×).
Token basis
Trimmed for cheaper models / lower iteration: image $0.10 · video/UGC $2.50 · animated 60s $7 · VSL-180s $10. Non-AI assets carry no token cost.
b-roll
AI b-roll $200 for 4 min ($28 editor + $72 generation). UGC b-roll $200 for the brief; creator fees ($150–500/creator, ~20 b-roll each) billed separately.

Concepts / month sizing

Testing budget %
20% of total spend allocated to creative testing.
CPA
Cost to acquire a new customer, default $100 (range $50–$200).
CPA test multiplier
2× default (range 1–6×) — spend per concept to read a valid test.
Concepts / month
total spend × testing % ÷ 30 ÷ CPA ÷ test multiplier × 4 weeks.
Terms90-day minimum commitment · $500 internal onboarding cost · guarantee miss = refund concept credits, no cash, waive 5%. Spend levels modeled: $30k → $3M/mo.
