03 Your role

Who we're looking for

You're a female dog owner, self-aware about being "diagnosably obsessed" with the smell problem, delivering this as a ranked product review.

  • The energy: high-energy, a little unhinged, comedic self-awareness.
  • Awareness stage: Solution-aware (tried everything else already).
  • Creative type: ranked product-comparison / tier-list format.
04 The angle

Your concept

"The Dog Smell Tier List", a ranked product-comparison format. She scores every product she's tried, candles, plug-ins, sprays, enzyme cleaners, mostly low, because they fight the air instead of the floor. Blyss is the only 10/10, because it addresses the real source.

05 How to film

Filming & setup

  • Self-filmed, not performed. Phone propped or handheld selfie style. Natural ad-libs and pauses welcome.
  • Talking head + your own POV b-roll: the dog walking his usual path (low camera), the spray-then-clock bit, the two-capfuls pour, the closing doorway shot.
  • The Blyss bottle should be visible in every action shot.
  • Setup: vertical (9:16), real home, bright natural light, unpolished and intimate.
06 Non-negotiables

Brand rules

  • Keep it self-filmed and real, not performed. Natural ad-libs and pauses are encouraged.
  • No spoken product benefit lists. If a benefit needs to be stated, keep it to on-screen text.
  • The Blyss bottle must be visible in every action shot.
  • Show real mopping/cleaning. Real home, not staged-perfect.
  • In the CTA, link the real site, there are a lot of fakes out there.
