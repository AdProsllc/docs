47 Skin — Quiz Funnel Creative Briefs

Creative Strategy & Brief Builder • Internal Use

The brief in one line

The quiz converts and brings in better customers at ~half the CAC. The problem is entirely at the ad level — the creative feeding the quiz isn't stopping the scroll. Quiz-ad link CTR is 1.65% vs 4.28% on other Meta. The fix is not the quiz. It's the ads.

Who we're talking to

PRIMARY • 60–70%

Protective Parent Petra — Mom of a teen with breakouts

38–48, $75K–$150K HHI, not price sensitive for her kid. Emotional arc: guilt & anxiety → relief & joy. Has tried everything; watches her teen withdraw, refuse photos, skip events. Acts fast (1–3 days) once trust is established.

VOC: "tried many different skin products for my 14-year-old," "cleared my son's skin when all else had failed," "noticed a difference within 2 weeks — clearer skin and more confidence."

Top objection: "My teen has already tried XYZ and nothing works."

SECONDARY • 20–30%

Struggling Sophie — Adult with hormonal / persistent breakouts

28–50. Arc: frustration & embarrassment → empowerment. Most acne products dry out her adult skin barrier; wins feel temporary as hormones cycle. Wants one product that handles active spots and scarring. Research-mode (reads reviews, Reddit).

Highest-CTR US TikTok angle (4.45%): "Pretty brown skin girlies who have adult hormonal acne / scars — THIS is the one." Brown & Black adult skin is critically underserved.

Top objection: "My skin is too sensitive — acne products make it worse, and everything stops working when my hormones shift."

Non-negotiable guardrails (brand voice & compliance)

Say this

  • spots, breakouts, blemishes, redness, bad skin, angry skin
  • Silver Chitoderm® (always ®, always the bond/combination — never silver or Chitoderm alone)
  • Formula 47, scar repair, clinically proven, real results, transformation
  • "$1/day" pricing reframe (50ml ≈ 60 days ≈ $31/mo)

Avoid this

  • "acne," "cure," "eliminates acne," "treatment," "prescription," "pharmaceutical" as brand claims (OK inside a clearly-marked customer quote)
  • On TikTok: use "mark repair," not "scar repair"
  • Obviously-AI faces; solid colored backgrounds (white only); over-polished beauty aesthetic; verbose science
Voice = "the good doctor": expert, empathetic, plain English, a little provocative. Headlines in sentence case, short, punchy, end with a full stop. Before/after always carries a results-vary disclaimer. US claims stay conservative until compliance docs land.

How to run the test

Batch 1 — cold acquisition (run first)

The two Tier-1s (01, 02) + one before/after (04) + the high-CTR adult-skin angle (03). All cold-traffic scroll-stoppers that isolate Principles 1–5. This is where we close the 1.65% → 4.28% CTR gap.

Batch 2 — expand the winners

05, 07, 08, 10 — secondary hooks and avatar variants once Batch 1 shows which open stops best.

Warm / retargeting only (not a cold test)

06 (guarantee-led) and 09 (testimonial) are MOF/BOF closers for traffic that already knows the brand — sequence them after the cold hooks land, don't pit them against the Tier-1s.

  • Hook discipline (the Mimika rule): the first 2 seconds — the visual + on-screen line — do the stopping. VO is support, not the hook. If a script reads like brand copy, cut it; let OST and b-roll carry it. Keep cold cuts to 12–18s.
  • Two production tracks in parallel: AI-VO+b-roll cell (01, 04, 05, 06, 09) reuses existing footage + a fresh quiz screen-recording; Animated-GTV cell (02, 03, 07, 08, 10) is fully in-house motion graphics. Neither needs a new UGC creator.
  • Record one clean capture of the live US quiz — it's the shared b-roll asset across half the concepts.
  • Hold variables steady: same audience + landing quiz; test hook/headline first (3 variants per brief), then format (AI-VO vs animated) on the winners.
  • Guarantee everywhere: mirror the 47-Day Guarantee on the quiz results page so ad → quiz → PDP stays congruent.
  • Primary metric: link CTR (target 3%+, gap-close toward 4.28%), then quiz-start and quiz-complete rates.
