4. Live Ad Library Findings

Apify Meta Ad Library scrapes completed. 28 active Luvra ads + 30 active Hismile ads pulled from US ad library. Below: the highest-leverage findings.

Finding 1: Hismile is not advertising toothpaste in the US right now

Every single top-impression Hismile ad pushes their iD Stain Whitening Mouthwash — not the toothpaste. The hook formula is visceral curiosity: “Gunk Removing Mouthwash 🫣😨,” “The most humbling mouthwash??,” “Instant Mouth Cleanse.” They’re running 23–80s video formats with the same stacked-benefit body copy.

Implication for Luvra: Less direct competitive pressure on toothpaste creative than we’d expect from a category leader. There is room to own toothpaste creative in the US ad library if Luvra moves now. Hismile’s emotional register (gross-out, shock, visceral curiosity) is the polar opposite of Luvra’s gentle/warm/clean register — two clear creative lanes.

Finding 2: Luvra creative is duplicative and trust-leaking

28 active ads collapse into roughly 3 message variants (similarAdCount = 3 per ad, meaning each ad has 3 near-duplicates). The lead hook is “Whiter Teeth. No Sensitivity” — the right wedge, but the rest of every ad is the same benefit list with the same emoji stack.

“Whiter Teeth. No Sensitivity. ✔️ Niacinamide-powered formula helps brighten gently without fluoride. ✔️ Strengthens enamel with mineral-rich care. ✔️ Natural, plant based formula for daily use. 🦷 Dentist Approved. ♻️ 30 Day Returns. 📦 Tracked Worldwide Shipping.” — Active Luvra ad (ID 851108580643473, repeated across 4+ creatives)

This is why CPM is high and CTR is low. Meta is paying full price to show the same message to people who’ve already seen it three times this week.

Finding 3: Two trust signals are actively broken in production

⚠ Fix today.

3A: Review count is now drifting in five places

  • Homepage: 2,900+ reviews
  • Homepage hero: 1,593 reviews
  • PDP Loox widget: 1,807 reviews
  • Active Meta ads: “25,000+ Happy Customers” — this is new and the most aggressive number
  • “Numbers Don’t Lie” PDP widget: broken (“1 %”)

Action: Pick one source of truth (likely the Loox count). Standardize. If 25,000+ is sales count not review count, label it that way (“25,000+ customers” ≠ “25,000+ reviews”).

3B: CTA button text is misconfigured

Across multiple Luvra ads, the CTA button text field contains the entire body copy (e.g. “Whiter Teeth. No Sensitivity✔️ Niacinamide-powered formula...”) instead of “Shop Now.” Likely a Shopify product feed / Meta catalog misconfiguration. The CTA button on these ads is unreadable. That alone is suppressing click-through.

Finding 4: Funnel coverage is TOF-only

Every Luvra ad I pulled is a TOF benefit-stacked product education piece. No MOF education ads, no BOF offer-led ads, no retargeting-specific creative visible in the top-impression set. That maps directly to the Low LTV / Low AOV pain points the client called out at intake.

Implication: Step 6 needs to build distinct creative for three audience states: problem-aware (TOF), product-aware (MOF), offer-aware (BOF). The Buy 2 Get 1 Free / 90-day transformation story belongs in BOF, not the front-end TOF feed.
