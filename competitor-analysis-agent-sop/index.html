1Purpose

Produce a daily competitive intelligence brief for Ad Pros leadership that surfaces only meaningful changes from competitor ad activity in the last 24 hours. The brief is read on mobile in 60 seconds.

The test: every line in the output must pass — would a reader know something they couldn't have guessed? If not, omit the line. Never write "stable," "no changes," "none," or similar non-statements.

2Input contract

3Tiering rules

Assign each brand to one of three tiers based on how many new ads they launched in the last 24h. Volume is the primary signal — everything else (offers, messaging, whitelisting) is detail layered on top.

Tier Threshold (new ads launched in 24h)
🔴 High 10+ new ads launched
🟡 Medium 5–10 new ads launched
⚪ Low <5 new ads launched. Includes blocked and no-data brands.

4Suppression rules per dimension

Each dimension is shown only if its trigger fires. Otherwise omit the line entirely.

Dimension Show when
Vitals row Always — this is the pulse check
Cadence >2x or <0.3x of 7-day rolling avg
Days-active signal ≥1 ad with 30d+ active turned off, OR >50% of library is <7d old, OR >14 days since last ad crossed 14d active, OR top-5 impression share shifted >15 points week-over-week
Format shift Any single format moved >10 points week-over-week
Offers New offer launched, OR discount depth changed ≥10 points, OR new discount mechanic
Messaging shift New theme entered top 3, OR existing theme dropped from top 3
Hooks ≥3 new hooks, OR new hook represents thematic shift
Headlines Net-new positioning angle (not a variation)
Landing pages Any new LP, OR existing LP materially modified
Whitelisting 🚨 Any newly detected partner. Always flag Ad Pros Creator Network overlaps.
Language New language detected, OR distribution shifted >20 points
Direct callouts 🚨 Always shown when present. Note ad count and days since last callout.

5Phrasing rules for surfaced dimensions

  • Days-active signals — plain-language observations, not bucket counts. Example: "4 ads with 14-30d active turned off — killing maturing creatives, not just tests."
  • Format shifts — include before → after percentages. Example: "Static 45% → 22%, Video 50% → 73%."
  • Cadence — include the multiplier vs. avg. Example: "5 launches vs. 1.8/day avg — 2.8x normal velocity."
  • Messaging shifts — say what replaced what. Example: "Scarcity replaces social proof as dominant angle."
  • Whitelisting — include follower tier (micro <100k / mid 100k–1M / macro 1M+). Flag Ad Pros Creator Network overlap with 🚨🚨.
  • Direct callouts — name the competitor and frequency context. Example: "AG1 named directly in 1 ad — first callout in 90d."

6Escalation triggers (🟡 Medium brands only)

Every 🟡 Medium brand block ends with → Escalate if: listing 2–3 binary, observable conditions that would bump them to 🔴 High tomorrow. Triggers must be specific and measurable.

✓ Good

"3+ whitelisted partners" · "any ad crosses 14d active" · "callout expands to 3+ ads"

✗ Bad

"they get more aggressive" · "if things change" · "continue monitoring"

10Tone rules

  • Terse. Read on mobile in 60 seconds.
  • No hedging — no appears to be, indicating, suggests. State what's happening.
  • Every observation must be specific and observable. No monitor or track or keep an eye on language.
  • No executive summary. No "watch next" speculation. The tier structure already ranks importance.
  • Use 🚨 sparingly — only for whitelisting detections, Creator Network overlaps, and direct competitor callouts.
