3. Usage Rights

The most important part of this deal. We lost a $1M-revenue creator ad last year because the usage rights expired. We're not letting that happen again. Our standard is perpetual rights — we'll never revoke, you'll never have to renew, and we both get the upside of content compounding over time.

What you grant Nurse Babes

  • Perpetual, worldwide paid-media usage rights on all delivered content + raw / unselected footage
  • Meta Partnership Ads whitelist authorization — we run ads through your handle
  • TikTok Spark Ads authorization — same on TikTok
  • Edit / derivative rights — we can recut, repurpose, and remix delivered footage into new ads without re-shooting
  • Name + likeness usage in paid creative for the term + perpetual on any signed assets

What we don't take

  • You retain ownership of your content for your own organic channels
  • You can use the work in your portfolio, reels, and personal marketing
  • We don't restrict your other paid partnerships outside of direct competitors (see Exclusivity below)

8. Brand Voice Guardrails

One thing we ask: certain language carries legal risk for the brand in paid advertising. Customer reviews are exempt — you can quote a customer saying anything — but content written or spoken in your voice can't make health/medical claims.

Phrases to avoid in your voice

  • "Pain-free" or "eliminates pain"
  • "Treats / cures / heals / fixes" — anything implying medical treatment
  • "Medical-grade" / "clinically proven" / "FDA approved"
  • "Plantar fasciitis cure" or similar condition-specific claims
  • "Doctor / podiatrist recommended" unless we have a specific named doctor on file

What you can absolutely say

  • "All-day comfort"
  • "Built for long days on your feet"
  • "Supportive" / "easy on your feet"
  • "Show up strong"
  • Your personal experience — how they feel, what they replaced, why you wear them

Why This Deal Structure

We've thought hard about how to structure creator partnerships that actually work long-term. The pattern we keep seeing across brands: one-off deals leave money on the table for both sides. Creators have to keep hunting for the next brand, brands have to keep recruiting and onboarding from scratch.

This deal is built on the opposite principle — we lock in a working relationship for a long-term arc, with performance triggers that compound as your content compounds.

If our content drives $50k/month, you make $5k+ in revenue share alone, on top of base. If it drives $100k/month, that's $10k+ for you — before counting the bonuses or affiliate side. Your incentives match ours.

And the ambassador track means you have a place in the brand that goes beyond producing videos. We want this relationship to be one that you'd brag about in 2 years.
