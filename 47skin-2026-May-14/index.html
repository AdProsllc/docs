02What Moved the Needle

Key actions, tests, and campaign changes this week

Increased spend on Best Performers ad set which has one of the highest ncROAS at scale in the account.

TOF Focus Expansion

Increased our focus of TOF even further, so we can start increasing Cost Caps slowly and steadily.

CBO Consolidation

Consolidation of CBO has continued proving itself to be effective, while we continue introducing more new creatives in batches.

New Traffic & First Time Conversion

Continuously increasing new traffic with higher NVPs. First Time Conversion optimized campaigns seem to be hitting it out the park with basically all creatives and batches launched.

WHAT WORKED THIS WEEK — AND WHY

  • Best Performers +30% Spend: Ad spend increased 30% on Best Performers given minimum spend adjustment. It now makes up 58% of the campaign’s spend.
  • Clicks Up 35% (23k → 31k): Added fuel to Cost Cap campaigns where we’ve successfully added budget increases.
  • Cost Cap Campaign 112% ncROAS: Already at 101%, went to 112% while doubling budget: $1,800 → $3,600 spend.
  • NVPs at 91%: TOF + First Conversion attribution campaigns crushing it. All adsets at or above 75% on TA (goal was 55%, now updated to 65%).

WHAT DIDN'T WORK — AND WHY

  • 2-Step Treatment Spend Halved: Next steps: continue adding more creatives to prove the bundled AOV theory for US.
  • Quiz Funnel Launch Delayed: Weren’t able to launch as quickly as planned.
  • No New Creative Winners: Brand new batch launched which should surface new ones soon.
  • TikTok Ads Not Yet Launched: Pixel issue resolved, should be implemented today.
