Blyss took a commodity category — floor cleaner — and re-framed it as a luxury fragrance product you happen to mop with. The Core Creative Architecture

Lead frame = the category-reveal surprise, not generic scent-luxury. The proven scroll mechanic is the reveal: "Not candles. Not diffusers. It's literally my floor cleaner." We run it at two emotional temperatures:

Warm traffic — Aspirational reveal

"Not candles, not diffusers — it's my floor cleaner." Model-home / luxury payoff. Retargeting & warm audiences.

Cold traffic — Crude problem-state

"Your house smells like dog and you're noseblind to it." The scroll-stopper — carries the untested whitespace lanes (cat, chemical-fear, jealous-neighbor).
