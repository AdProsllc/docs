    02 · READ-OUT

    What the data says

    Read-out

    • Blended NC ROAS is 1.15x on $956,239 of spend across 9 LPs. Headline metric is positive but thin — the spread underneath is what matters.
    • The top funnel page (try.valorrea.com/cmiar4dk...) is the single biggest risk: $446,309 spent at 1.04x. It's eating ~47% of budget at near break-even. Moving this LP from 1.04x → 1.20x would lift total NC revenue by ~$71,409.
    • Highest ROAS at meaningful scale: the Bible Study Workbook for Kids PDP at 1.39x on $143,115. Strong candidate for scaling spend — CVR is 3.77% on 83,725 sessions.
    • Two pages should be paused or rebuilt: /pages/jennystory (0.58x on $4,680) and /pages/susan (0.29x on $2,486). Combined drag is $3,697 in net loss over the window.
    • Listicle LPs deliver: both /listicle-kids (1.40x) and /listicle-adults (1.13x) outperform their direct-PDP counterparts. Worth doubling down on this format and testing a third variant.

    07 · METHODOLOGY

    How this was built

    Methodology

    • Data window: Last 90 days, click-date attribution
    • Source: Meta Ads spend × Triple Attribution (Triple Whale) for NC revenue, orders, sessions
    • NC ROAS = New-Customer Revenue ÷ Spend
    • NC = New Customer — first-time purchaser, attributed via Triple Whale's deterministic model
    • Tiers: High (≥ 1.30x) · Medium (1.00–1.29x) · Low (< 1.00x)
    • Scope: Landing page roll-up only. Ad-level diagnostics and UTM coverage available on request.
