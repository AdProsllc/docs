02Housekeeping

nCAC barely moved ($611 to $627) on about the same spend — the account itself didn't get worse.

Why it happened

People clicked more but bought less

The ads got cheaper clicks and more of them, but fewer of those visitors bought. That points to something on the site, not the ads or the audience.

Holiday sale wearing off

The dip lines up with the holiday sale ending (around May 27–28), and it showed up on every channel, not just Meta. nCAC came back to normal by May 31–June 1.

What we think this means

It points more at the landing page than the ads — but it's early

Since the ads were getting cheaper clicks while fewer people bought, the issue may sit on the landing page rather than the creative. We can't say that for sure — we haven't launched new ads in a while, so there's a lot we still want to test. The quiz landing page test should help us learn more.

Meta — next

Keep NMN spend flat until nCAC settles

No spending more into a slow stretch.

Send NMN traffic to the new quiz landing page test

Rolling out a few ad sets at a time so we don't reset the ads' learning.

Build a quiz-completion event in Meta

There isn't one yet, so we'll create it, let it gather data, and only then consider having Meta optimize toward it (quiz finish rate: NMN 3.2%, BPC 16%).
