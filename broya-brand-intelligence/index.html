4. Top Objections (Creative Hurdles)

These must be addressed — directly or indirectly — in ad creative. Ignoring them burns budget.

🍖 Bone Broth Objections

#1 — Taste
"Tastes like glue" · "smells weird" · "too gelatinous"
Creative must show real people enjoying it — not just talking about benefits.
#2 — Price
"Too expensive for daily use" · "I can make this at home for pennies"
Cost-per-serving framing ($3–4/day), quality sourcing proof, time-saving angle.
#3 — Convenience
"Too messy to prepare" · "I forget to take it"
RTD/single-serve format angle. Habit-stacking with morning routine.
#4 — Skepticism
"Just glorified stock" · "no real science behind this"
Third-party validation, before/after testimonials, expert endorsement.

✨ Tallow Skincare Objections

#1 — Pore Clogging (~20% of Reviews)
"Will this break me out?" · "I have acne-prone skin"
Must address head-on. Comedogenic rating data, testimonials from acne-prone skin.
#2 — No Scientific Proof
"There's no peer-reviewed research on tallow skincare"
Lipid science angle, sebum compatibility data, expert/dermatologist voice.
#3 — Smell
"Smells like a steakhouse" · "I can't wear this to work"
Address with "subtle, natural scent" framing. Or pure/unscented positioning.
#4 — Affiliate/MLM Trust
"These influencers are all paid affiliates — how do I know it works?"
Earned media, organic UGC, before/after photos, unboxing-style authenticity.

The "inside-out beauty" angle — nourish your skin from the inside (bone broth collagen) and outside (tallow balm) — is completely unclaimed. No competitor can make this claim. This is Broya's moat.
