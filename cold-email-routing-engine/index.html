    Cold Email — Ad-Library Routing Engine

    How we turn the Apollo list into personalized outreach at scale

    The Engine

    Every email anchored to a real fact about their ads

    A cold email workflow for the AdPros lead list. Instead of "we run ads for DTC brands," every message opens with something true and current about the prospect's own Meta ads — pulled live from the Meta Ad Library, read for where their creative is lacking, and matched to the AdPros offer that fixes it. The ad data is both the qualifier and the personalization engine.

    01 · PIPELINE

    Five stages, lead to send

    Each lead flows through the same five steps. Stages 2–3 are the moat; the rest is execution.

    1

    Validate

    Re-verify the email at the top of the run. Apollo already verified 999/1000, but verification rots — a freshness pass protects domain reputation.

    2

    Has ads?

    Resolve the Meta page and check the ad library. Actively running → continue. Nothing running → drop or park. This is the real qualifier Apollo can't give us.

    3

    Read & bucket

    Extract the signals from their live creative and sort the lead into the bucket that describes where their ads are lacking.

    4

    Route to campaign

    The bucket maps to one Instantly campaign and the AdPros offer that fills the gap.

    5

    Personalize

    The pipeline writes one true sentence about their account — the only per-lead writing job. Everything else is a proven template.

    02 · LAYER 1

    The signals we extract per lead

    Six numbers, all confirmed pulling cleanly from the Meta Ad Library API. Everything downstream routes on these.

    Active ad count

    Spend tier / sophistication — the qualifier Apollo couldn't give us.

    Static % vs video %

    Format skew. Decides which of the two offers fills the gap.

    % of ads > 90 days old

    Creative fatigue — budget being spent on tired ads.

    Distinct hooks ÷ ad count

    Concept concentration. Few ideas across many ads = fragile.

    Recent-launch flag

    New product detected in ad copy — a timely, flattering hook.

    Domain-matched at all?

    The gate. No matched ads = not advertising = drop.

    Each bucket maps a signal to a pain point, an offer, and an Instantly campaign.

    Offer correlates with spend naturally: AI Creative's retainer scales $1K→$5K with spend, so whales land in its higher tiers while thin accounts get Static's $500 door. The format gap picks the offer; spend tier tells the rep which retainer to quote.

    04 · LAYER 3

    The routing algorithm

    A lead can trip several triggers (a whale can also be fatigued). Precedence makes routing deterministic — first match wins, so every lead lands in exactly one campaign.

    1. no active matched ads → DROP (dormant nurture later) 2. active ads < 10 → Thin Account → Build Depth (Static) 3. video ≥70% & static ≤20% → Static Gap → Static White Space (Static) 4. static ≥70% & video ≤25% → Motion Gap → Add Motion (AI Creative) 5. ≥20% of ads >90 days old → Fatigue Tail → Fatigue Tail (lighter format) 6. hooks ÷ ads < 0.15 → One-Trick → More Angles (lighter format) 7. else → Competent Operator → Net-New Winners (lighter format + launch hook)
    Format-gap rules sit above fatigue/one-trick because format skew routes the offer most confidently. If we'd rather lead with the most urgent pain regardless of offer, we flip them. Tunable once reply data is in.

    06 · EXECUTION

    How it runs in Instantly

    Each bucket is one Instantly campaign — its own lead list, schedule, and 4-touch sequence. The copy is written once per campaign; only the per-lead variables change.

    TouchDayJob
    10The observation — the personalized insight line. Soft CTA (free audit).
    23The stakes — what the pain costs at their volume + the 30-day money-back guarantee.
    37The proof — one comparable result + the sample-ads link.
    411The breakup — short, leaves the audit on the table.

    Per-lead variables the pipeline injects

    { first_name, company, campaign_id (= bucket), insight_line, ← the one true sentence about their ads offer_name, ← Static or AI Creative format_stat, ← "84% video / 16% static" hook_or_product ← "strawberry stick packs" / "two hooks" }

    The agent's entire per-lead writing job is insight_line. Everything else is a proven, hand-tuned template for that bucket.

    07 · FUNNEL & NEXT

    Funnel math & what's next

    Expected funnel

    Roughly 40–70% of the 1,000 are expected to come back as actively advertising. So this batch likely yields 500–700 qualified, ad-personalized leads; the rest get parked.

    1,000 is a starter batch

    At 1,000+/month ongoing, the pipeline needs a continuous sourcing arm feeding the same engine, or it runs dry in week two. Decide v1 scope: this list only, or sourcing included.

    Build note: brand-name search is noisy (“Happy V” matched romance-novel spam). The reliable resolution is to search the domain token (“happyv”) then keep only ads whose landing domain matches the lead's website. Fatigue stats require pulling the full active set, not just the most recent page.

    Open decisions before build

    A

    Scan distribution

    Run 25–30 leads to see the real bucket spread and the true "actively advertising" hit rate.

    B

    v1 scope

    Work this list only, or include continuous sourcing for sustained 1,000+/mo volume.

    C

    Launch buckets

    Start with the 3–4 highest-frequency buckets; add the rest as volume justifies separate sequences.
