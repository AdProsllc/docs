Pricing Model — Service Combos

Scenario: $1M / month managed spend · Performance-discount tiers

Executive Summary

Six service combos. One pricing model. Built to scale with spend.

Ad Pros now operates against a unified pricing architecture: flat base retainers for fixed-deliverable services, tiered variable rates for spend-scaling services, and a performance-discount mechanic tied to new customer ROAS. This document maps six combo plans against a $1M / month managed-spend scenario.

Key insights

  • Default plan is Growth. Most prospects spending $250K–$750K/mo will land here at ~$69K/mo on $1M of spend (6.9% of spend).
  • CPN is your second-largest revenue line on Scale and Breakthrough. 23.5% of Scale and 26.3% of Breakthrough comes from CPN. Whoever owns CPN owns major revenue.
  • Media Buying is capped at $20K variable / month. This is buyer-friendly but materially shifts unit economics — uncapped would be $30K at $1M spend.
  • Creative Production scales on 80% of spend (the non-CPN portion), recognizing CPN spend has its own production already covered by the CPN fee.
  • Performance discount at -100% (4x+ nc-ROAS) drops Breakthrough from $123.6K to $49.6K — a 60% cut. Consider capping max discount at -75% to preserve operational margin.
Strategic positioning line Our pricing has two layers. A base retainer that covers the team and the AI infrastructure. A variable fee tied to your spend that scales our work with your scale — and shrinks as your new customer ROAS improves. We win when you win.

Supporting Info

Observations, mix strategy, and what to lock down next

Observations on line-item economics

  • In Growth (default plan), Creative Production is the biggest single line at 42% — bigger than Media Buying at 36%. Driven by the Media Buying cap at $20K. If Media Buying should be the headline service economically, the cap needs to lift or remove.
  • In Scale and Breakthrough, CPN is the second or third largest line (23.5% Scale, 26.3% Breakthrough). Whoever owns CPN is operating a major revenue driver — staffing and operational rigor on CPN delivery should reflect that.
  • Creative Strategy at $10K is 8-15% of full-stack combos. Material but not dominant. If the deliverable matures, pricing has room to flex upward.
  • The intel layer (Intel + AI Ads Library + Agentic Workflows + Dashboards) sums to under 3% of any full-service combo. This is the moat — disproportionately low fee for what is structurally the differentiator. Sells via wedge offer (Intelligence-Only at $1,399) and bundles into all higher tiers.

Build dependencies blocking sale of these combos

Per the latest service overview, several services are flagged "Stuck" or "Doesn't Exist." Combos blocked:

  • Combo 3 (Creative Engine) blocked by: Creative Strategy (Stuck)
  • Combo 4 (Growth) blocked by: Creative Strategy (Stuck), AI Landing Pages (Doesn't Exist)
  • Combo 5 (Scale) blocked by: above + CPN (Stuck), AI Organic Content (Doesn't Exist)
  • Combo 6 (Breakthrough) blocked by: all of the above

Sellable today: Combo 1 (Media Only) and Combo 2 (Intelligence-Only). Unblocking Creative Strategy + shipping AI Landing Pages v1 opens 80% of the prospect funnel.

What to lock down before publishing this as a sales artifact

  • Define the deliverables per combo. Each combo needs a one-pager: deliverables, cadence, ad-unit capacity, LP templates included, strategy workshop count, intel memo frequency.
  • Define "new customer" attribution methodology for the nc-ROAS discount tiers. Tied to client's first-time-purchaser flag in Shopify or attribution platform. Lock the methodology in the MSA.
  • Define CPN spend allocation. Currently assumed at 10% of total media spend on Scale, 15% on Breakthrough. In practice this is client-specific — make it part of the onboarding rules document.
  • Decide on max discount cap. Recommend −75% as the max to preserve operational margin at breakthrough nc-ROAS levels.
  • Decide on graduation logic. What automatically moves a client from Growth → Scale → Breakthrough? Spend thresholds? Channel count? Make the path explicit.
