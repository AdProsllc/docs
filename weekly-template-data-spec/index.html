The entire BigQuery dependency

Only 3 of 8 sections read from BigQuery — Northstar, channel stats, and creatives. They reduce to three windowable, client-filtered queries returning a few raw columns. The worker computes every ratio, delta, sparkline, and color flag from these.

QueryGrainRaw columns neededFeeds
A — Accountclient × weeknc_revenue, total_spendNorthstar (02)
B — Channelclient × channel × weekspend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenueChannel stats (04–06)
C — Creativeclient × creative × weekcreative_id, creative_type, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenueCreatives (07)
