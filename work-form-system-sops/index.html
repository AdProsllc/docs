The Work Form System — SOPs

16 standard operating procedures, one per tab

Every repeatable procedure in Matthew Larsen's productivity system, broken into 16 SOPs. One tab each. Run them as checklists — start on the last tab, Implementation.

Baseline: four Deep Work Hours per day, completed within ~5–6 hours of waking. SOPs 01–08 are the daily and weekly core. SOPs 09–16 are advanced — don't start them until the core has run three consecutive months.

SOP 01

Setup

One-time · ~1 hour · 22 steps

  1. Prepare a quiet, dark, comfortable Work Room; desk holds only what the work needs
  2. Strip the Information Diet: no short-form, feeds, news, porn, chronically negative people
  3. Create a recurring calendar block protecting the first five hours after waking
  4. Put a physical 60-minute timer on the desk
  5. Put a physical distraction counter on the desk + a pad to log causes
  6. Install a tab limiter, max 2–3 tabs
  7. Install and schedule desktop app blockers; kill feeds; disable notifications
  8. Set up phone blockers (Brick + Opal + Screen Time); phone out of bedroom and out of the work room
  9. Write and print the 10X Rule document
  10. Write Top Five Goals — each with a number, a deadline, and a reason
  11. Write the Motivation List — roughly 60% away-from, 40% toward
  12. Write Version 1.0 vs 2.0 across beliefs, character traits, skills
  13. Write Thought Habits for the predictable moments you slow down or make excuses
  14. Print all five documents and post them beside the desk
  15. Hang a large physical calendar in peripheral vision
  16. Stack sticky notes + pen beside the timer; clear wall space for the note wall
  17. Build and bookmark the Work Form with every field and checkbox
  18. Pipe every Work Form submission into a spreadsheet; bookmark it
  19. Form the accountability group; automate a notification on every submission
  20. Build the leaderboard: total hours + time-to-first-hour
  21. Create and pin the Worry List; dump everything currently weighing on you
  22. Build the War Map: goals → yearly → quarterly → monthly → 3–5 daily deliverables
SOP 02

Morning Routine

~30 min · before Hour One · 11 steps

  1. Water, creatine, protein shake, video and stretch routine were laid out last night
  2. Treadmill 20 minutes — ~3 mph, 8–12% incline
  3. Drink 1,000 mL of water while walking
  4. Take creatine if you already use it
  5. 30g protein, premade shake — no cooking
  6. Watch or listen to a pre-saved motivational video
  7. Plan all four Deep Work Hours — one specific deliverable each
  8. Stretch five minutes
  9. Write the sticky note: date + "Morning Routine Complete" → wall
  10. Take the small reward and consciously tie it to finishing the routine
  11. Sit down, start the timer, begin Hour One
SOP 03

Deep Work Hour

Repeat ×4 · the core loop · 9 steps

  1. Rush Hour One — minimize the gap between waking and finishing it
  2. Monday: Hour One is the Anti-Worry Hour
  3. Pick one specific deliverable from the morning plan — a noun, not a category
  4. Reset the distraction counter to zero
  5. Start the 60-minute timer and begin
  6. Every distraction: click the counter and write down the cause
  7. Do not stop because the work got hard, boring, or frustrating
  8. Work until the timer hits zero
  9. Run the End-of-Hour SOP
SOP 04

End of Hour

~3 min · after every hour · 9 steps

  1. Write the sticky note: date, hour number, what you completed, distraction count
  2. Place it on the wall — group by project
  3. Read the 10X Rule
  4. Read Top Five Goals — did this hour support one? Will the next?
  5. Read the Motivation List out loud
  6. Read the 2.0 Version and look at the accumulating proof
  7. Read Thought Habits; deploy the right one if you're fading
  8. Submit the Work Form immediately — never batch four at the end of the day
  9. Hour One only: log wake time, completion time, and the delta
SOP 05

Between Hours

~10 min · recover and restart · 8 steps

  1. Start a 10-minute timer the moment the break begins
  2. Go to your designated between-hours spot
  3. Pick one or two recovery actions: stretch, 250–500 mL water, slow treadmill walk, tidy the desk
  4. Take the small reward — consciously tied to the hour you just finished
  5. Run the Self-Improvement Loop
  6. Say it: "I need to start the next hour as quickly as possible"
  7. Back at the desk inside 10–15 minutes
  8. Do not let the reward turn a 10-minute break into 40
SOP 06

Self-Improvement Loop

~20 sec · between every hour · 6 steps

  1. Tick the premade checklist: sticky note, 10X Rule, goals, motivation list, 2.0 version, thought habits, Work Form
  2. List 20 different ways you could improve — actions, not outcomes
  3. Name your single biggest constraint from the previous hour
  4. Fix it before the next hour if it's fixable now
  5. Compute the efficiency ratio: total allowed time ÷ total time awake
  6. Log it and try to move it toward 100% over time
SOP 07

Night Routine

3–5 min · closes the loop · 8 steps

  1. Clean the desk; everything back where it belongs; kill the visual noise
  2. Log wake time and Hour Four completion time
  3. Compute total time taken and the efficiency ratio (5:10 booked ÷ actual)
  4. Record it in the efficiency spreadsheet
  5. Lay out shoes, water, creatine, headphones; set the alarm
  6. Phone out of the bedroom
  7. Nasal strip, white noise, blackout blinds, cold room
  8. If you can't sleep, read — don't reach for the phone
SOP 08

Anti-Worry Hour

Monday · Hour One · 6 steps

  1. Open the pinned Worry List
  2. Close everything that can be closed in a single action
  3. Initiate anything that needs a response from someone else — email, call, booking, payment
  4. Add anything new that surfaced over the weekend
  5. Run it Mon/Wed/Fri instead if the backlog is genuinely out of control
  6. Monday morning specifically — businesses are open and replies come back same-day
SOP 09

Weekly Systems Block

Sat or Sun · 4 hours · 6 steps

  1. Pick one recurring task that sits outside your Big Three
  2. Do the task — but document it while you do it
  3. Produce the SOP, checklist, template, or Loom
  4. Train the person who will own it
  5. Hand it off and confirm it ran without you next cycle
  6. Repeat with the next task the following week
SOP 10

War Map

Quarterly build · daily use · 6 steps

  1. Tab one: the full year, with every Top Five Goal plotted by quarter
  2. Colour-code by goal, business, or major project; define the key
  3. One tab per month: monthly objectives, a day-by-day calendar, notes
  4. Break annual → quarterly → monthly → daily
  5. Three to five specific deliverables per day. Never more than five
  6. Pull tomorrow's hours straight off the War Map during the night routine
SOP 11

Rule of Three

Quarterly · effectiveness audit · 7 steps

  1. List every single task you perform in a typical week or month — including the small ones
  2. If you could only do ONE of these all day, which produces the most value? Circle it
  3. If only two — what's second? Circle it
  4. If only three — what's third? Circle it
  5. Those are your Big Three; roughly 90% of your value
  6. Systematically delegate, downsize, automate, or eliminate everything else
  7. Reinvest every recovered hour into the Big Three
SOP 12

100-Hour Sprint

Advanced · 2 weeks, temporary · 8 steps

  1. Only after 3+ consecutive months at four hours/day
  2. Pick one project — your Magnum Opus, not twenty unrelated things
  3. Define exactly what "done" looks like at day 14
  4. Target ~7 hours 9 minutes/day for 14 days
  5. Track: day, hours today, cumulative, remaining, what you completed
  6. Review the sheet nightly and set tomorrow's target
  7. No rounding up. No credit for time that wasn't deep work
  8. Return to the four-hour baseline when it ends
SOP 13

Version Upgrade

Every 6–12 months · 7 steps

  1. Write Version N.0: your actual current beliefs, character traits, skills — what your behaviour proves, not what sounds good
  2. Write Version N+1.0: the beliefs to adopt, traits to develop, skills to acquire
  3. Cap it at five per category — more dilutes it
  4. Describe the NEXT version, not the final one
  5. Print it and post it beside the desk
  6. Read it after every Deep Work Hour
  7. When the new version feels normal, write the one after it
SOP 14

Volume Expansion

Advanced · 4 → 8 hours · 8 steps

  1. Get your spouse's genuine buy-in first — trade something concrete for the time
  2. Hold five hours/day for two weeks before moving to six
  3. Then six → seven → eight, same gradual cadence
  4. Structure as two blocks: the original four, then the extra
  5. Four hours stays the baseline. Everything above is extra
  6. Optional: stack hours (90 or 120 min continuous) once stamina allows
  7. Stop expanding the moment quality drops — more chair time isn't more work
  8. Cap at eight. Beyond that it stops being sustainable forever
SOP 15

Accountability Group + Leaderboard

Weekly upkeep · 7 steps

  1. Recruit 3–5 people actually running the system
  2. Everyone submits into one shared spreadsheet
  3. Zapier fires an email/SMS to the group on every submission
  4. Leaderboard on tab one, auto-calculated
  5. Track total hours: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual, all-time
  6. Track time-to-first-hour across the same periods
  7. Bookmark it so the standings are always one click away
SOP 16

Efficiency Ratio

Nightly · the honesty metric · 7 steps

  1. Total allowed time = 30 min morning routine + 4×60 min + 4×10 min = 5h10m
  2. Total time taken = wake time → Hour Four completion
  3. Efficiency = allowed ÷ taken
  4. Example: 5:10 ÷ 5:47 = 89.3%
  5. Log it in the efficiency spreadsheet every night
  6. Anything under 100% is procrastination, drift, long breaks, or interruptions
  7. Total time taken should fall and the ratio should climb, month over month
