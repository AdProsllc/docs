Comparison framing unlocked previously-fatigued cold audiences

Comparison hooks > aspirational creative for cold audiences in this category

Worth ~3 more variant tests over the next 2–3 weeks. Different competitors, different value props, same structural format.

Retargeting fatigue threshold is ~frequency 3.8 in this account

Setting this as our automated refresh trigger going forward. No more reactive refreshes after we've already lost a week of efficiency.

Why it happened

Account was too thin per ad set — learning phases never completed

23 active ad sets at $14K/week meant ~$85/day each — below the 50-conversion learning threshold for most. Pruning concentrated spend so survivors actually exited learning.

●

Concentration moved spend to ad sets with proven CTR

Auction quality is endogenous — TikTok rewards consistency at the ad-set level. We'd been spreading too thin to signal consistency.

●

TikTok algorithm punishes broad-thin structures more than Meta does

Confirmed by the 14% CPM drop with no audience change. Platform-specific structural lesson.

What we learned

TikTok rewards concentration over breadth in this account

Operating principle going forward: cap active ad sets at 6. Above that, performance degrades non-linearly.

Creative batch turnover needs to compress to 7 days, not 14

TikTok creative half-life is shorter than Meta's. Adjusting our creative-production sprint cadence to match.

Creative analysis

●

Top creative pattern

All three top performers lead with a direct "us vs. them" or "before/after" comparison structure in the first 1.5 seconds. The audience is responding to specificity — abstract aspiration is underperforming on every platform.

●

Key creative learning

Audience has matured beyond category awareness. They know skincare brands exist; they're now choosing between them. Our creative needs to do the comparison work for them, not assume they'll do it themselves.

●

Next creative test

Three new variants of the comparison structure: (a) C014's hook against a specific named competitor, (b) before/after with stronger before-photo, (c) ingredient-spec comparison ("ours has X, theirs has Y").
