What happened

●

Comparison-hook creative (C014) scaled to $1.8K/day

Now driving 38% of new-customer revenue on Meta. Was at $200/day learning on Monday.

●

Retargeting frequency reset from 4.2 → 2.1

Shipped fresh creative Tuesday. Same audience, doubled CTR within 48 hours.

●

Paused 2 underperforming prospecting creatives

Both had CPA >$50 over the prior 7 days. Reclaimed $4.2K/week of budget.

Why it happened

●

Comparison framing unlocked previously-fatigued cold audiences

Different cognitive frame than the lifestyle creative we'd been running for 6 weeks. CPM dropped 12% as relevance score climbed.

●

Refreshed retargeting removed the "I've seen this 5 times" signal

CTR on retargeting audiences went 0.94% → 1.81% on the same audience definition. Pure creative effect — no audience or budget change.

●

Concentration on validated winners cut auction waste

Pausing the bottom 2 creatives funneled spend to the top 4. Account-level CPM dropped 11%.

What we learned

Comparison hooks > aspirational creative for cold audiences

Worth ~3 more variant tests over the next 2–3 weeks. Different competitors, different value props, same structural format.

Retargeting fatigue threshold is ~frequency 3.8 in this account

Setting this as our automated refresh trigger going forward.

What happened

●

Paused 11 ad sets with ROAS < 1.4 over rolling 14 days

Active ad-set count dropped from 23 to 12. Top 4 now receive 90% of spend.

●

CPM dropped −14% as we exited low-quality auctions

Pure structural effect — concentrating spend on validated placements lifted relevance scores.

●

Creative refresh cycle audit completed

Our 14-day batch turnover is too slow for TikTok. Top creative lifecycle is closer to 7 days.

Why it happened

●

Account was too thin per ad set — learning phases never completed

23 active ad sets at $14K/week meant ~$85/day each — below the 50-conversion learning threshold.

●

Concentration moved spend to ad sets with proven CTR

Auction quality is endogenous — TikTok rewards consistency at the ad-set level.

●

TikTok algorithm punishes broad-thin structures more than Meta does

Confirmed by the 14% CPM drop with no audience change. Platform-specific structural lesson.

What we learned

TikTok rewards concentration over breadth in this account

Operating principle: cap active ad sets at 6. Above that, performance degrades non-linearly.

Creative batch turnover needs to compress to 7 days, not 14

TikTok creative half-life is shorter than Meta's. Adjusting our creative-production sprint cadence to match.

Creative analysis

●

Top creative pattern

All three top performers lead with a direct "us vs. them" or "before/after" comparison structure in the first 1.5 seconds. The audience is responding to specificity — abstract aspiration is underperforming on every platform.

●

Key creative learning

Audience has matured beyond category awareness. They're choosing between brands now. Our creative needs to do the comparison work for them.

●

Next creative test

Three new variants of the comparison structure: (a) C014's hook against a specific named competitor, (b) before/after with stronger before-photo, (c) ingredient-spec comparison.
