Fewer ad sets meant budget concentrated on winners faster, exiting the learning phase 2 days earlier than prior week.

●

Hook rate on video creatives spiked

3-second video hold rate hit 38%, signaling strong pattern-interrupt at the feed level before the product reveal.

Social proof with a visible person speaking to camera consistently beats aspirational lifestyle imagery for NC ROAS at this price point.

03 Google · diagnosis

Why it happened

●

Meta halo effect on branded search

When Meta drives strong awareness, branded search volume follows within 3–5 days. This week confirmed that cross-channel flywheel pattern.

●

PMax rewarded fresh creative inputs

Google's system had been recycling the same assets for 6 weeks. New copy variants gave it new combinations to test, improving ad relevance scores.

Next week

1

Add product-specific landing pages to PMax

Segment top-3 SKUs into individual asset groups to give the algorithm tighter product-to-query matching.

2

Test +15% bid on Brand terms

Impression share still at 72% — room to capture more branded queries before competitors do.

What we learned

Cross-channel attribution matters more than single-channel ROAS

Google's NC ROAS looks modest in isolation, but it's capturing demand Meta created. Judging Google purely on its own numbers understates its value.

PMax creative refresh is maintenance, not optional

Asset groups degrade after ~6 weeks without new inputs. Monthly refreshes should become a standing process, not a reactive one.
