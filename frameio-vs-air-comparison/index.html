    03 — Scaling to +10 Clients
    The cost cliff that matters most

    Adding 10 clients doesn't just mean more work — it means more seats, more workspaces, and more storage. The two platforms respond to that growth very differently.

    Frame.io Cost Scenarios

    ~8 clients, 10 internal seats (Team)$250/mo
    At full Team plan capacity (15 seats)$375/mo
    +10 clients → 18+ seats neededEnterprise (est. $800–1,500+/mo)
    Separate workspace per clientEnterprise only
    External reviewers (clients reviewing)Free as Reviewers
    ⚠️ The moment you exceed 15 internal seats or need separate client workspaces, you're in Enterprise territory with opaque custom pricing. The jump from $375/mo transparent → $800–1,500+/mo custom is a significant unknown for budget planning.

    Air Cost Scenarios

    Current load — Business plan~$600–1,100/mo (flat)
    +10 clients, same team headcountNo seat cost increase
    More asset volume / AI usageCredit top-up or next plan tier
    Client access (all reviewers/stakeholders)Always free — unlimited
    Separate Brand Portal per clientIncluded — no upgrade needed
    ✅ Air's flat-seat model means adding clients doesn't compound your bill. The only scaling variable is credit consumption as asset libraries grow — and that's gradual, not a cliff. Much more predictable for agency growth planning.

    05 — Verdict
    The recommendation

    Choose Frame.io if…

    • Video production is your primary deliverable and editors live in Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro
    • You need Camera-to-Cloud from live shoots
    • Forensic or session-based watermarking is a client security requirement
    • You want the simplest per-seat pricing and won't grow beyond 15 internal seats
    • Pure video review UX is a non-negotiable for your clients

    Choose Air if…

    • You produce mixed creative — images, video, design, copy — across many clients
    • Scaling to 10+ more clients without paying per additional seat matters
    • You want AI tools to generate ad variants and repurpose approved assets in-platform
    • Finding and reusing past creative across the library is part of your workflow
    • You need per-client Brand Portals without jumping to Enterprise pricing
    Recommended for AdPros at Scale
    Air

    As an agency producing mixed creative for multiple clients and planning to grow, Air's unlimited-seat model, DAM capabilities, AI Canvas, and Brand Portals give better long-term economics and workflow coverage. The higher flat monthly cost is offset by no per-seat growth penalty and no forced Enterprise migration. That said, Frame.io remains the right call if video review + Adobe NLE integration is the core workflow — and the two are not mutually exclusive. Some agencies run both: Frame.io for active video production feedback, Air as the long-term creative library.

    Not mutually exclusive Frame.io's base Team plan runs $250–375/mo. Air Business runs ~$600–1,100/mo. Running both is a real option and costs less than Frame.io Enterprise alone — worth considering if video production feedback via NLE plugins is a genuine daily workflow need.
