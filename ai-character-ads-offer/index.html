Questions

Common questions, answered.

What's the difference between a concept and a variation?

A concept is a group of ideas built around one character — a specific persona with a defined visual style, demographic, and energy. A variation is a different script or hook for that same character. Different style or character means a different concept.

Example: One concept might be a 30-year-old fitness enthusiast. The 4 variations would be the same character delivering different hooks — pain point, social proof, before/after, problem/solution.

What's different about the Premium tier?

Premium is for brands that want fully bespoke characters, not AI-templated ones. You get unlimited revisions, faster turnaround (5–7 days guaranteed), a dedicated strategist with a direct Slack channel, your choice of visual style per concept (2D, 3D, anime, claymation), 4K output, and all aspect ratios plus GIFs and statics. Performance reporting with iteration recommendations is included.

Pricing is $1,000 per concept (each concept includes 8 variations). You choose how many concepts to order.

Will Meta flag AI-generated ads?

No. Meta, Google, and TikTok all use generative AI in their own ad tools and platforms accept AI creative without restriction. Standard ad policies apply — no false health claims, no misleading offers — the same as any other ad. We've never had an account flagged for using AI creative.

15–30 second video ads with AI voiceover, captions, and sound design. Starter pack includes 9:16 (vertical) and 1:1 (square). Performance adds 4:5. Premium delivers all aspect ratios plus GIFs and statics, in 4K.

What if my brand has a strong existing visual style?

Different visual styles count as different concepts. If you want one character in flat 2D and another in 3D, that's two concepts. Premium tier lets you specify a style per concept (2D, 3D, anime, claymation, etc.). We adapt to your brand, you don't adapt to ours.
