Creator Engine — Business Model

The refined operating thesis — North Star, unit economics, portfolio, constraint chain, and pre-mortem

The Creator Engine business model, refined through a 20-question interview on August 29, 2026. This is the operating thesis: what the North Star actually means in net terms, the portfolio shape that hits it, the constraint chain with hire triggers, and the risks that kill it. It is the business model the Master Performance System tracks.

1. North Star — restated in net terms

$30M/moAttributed revenue for brands ($1M/day)
$3M/moGross to Ad Pros (10% of attributed)
$1.5M/moNet after 50/50 creator split (~$50K/day)
60Clients at ~$500K avg attributed

Pricing: $5K/month or 5% of attributed revenue net of creator split — whichever is greater, never stacked. The retainer is a floor that matters only during ramp; past ~$100K/month attributed, the rev share governs. Meta counts the money: attribution setting frozen at signing, ROAS target agreed upfront, dispute process defined so it never becomes a relationship blow-up. The headline-to-keep ratio is 20:1 — worth remembering when the $1M/day number is quoted.

The 20 L/XL accounts carry two-thirds of the money; the 40 S/M accounts are the pipeline that produces them. S-tier exists as an audition, not an annuity: cross $100K attributed by day 90 or exit. 4. Churn — the kill zone

Churn concentrates at the 90-day and 6-month marks, in brands doing sub-$100K/month attributed. The single highest-leverage survival OKR: time-to-$100K-attributed < 90 days. Once a client crosses $100K they exit the kill zone and become an annuity. Client approval latency (scripts, creators, videos) is the leading indicator of churn — a brand taking 6 days to approve at week 3 is telegraphing the day-90 cancellation.

Retention owner: Danna (interim); a dedicated retention hire is a priority. Goal: 8% → 5% monthly. At 8%, holding a 60-book requires ~58 replacements/year; at 5%, ~36 — equivalent to adding half a closer without hiring anyone.

5. Sales motion & constraint chain

Binding constraint: packaging → pipeline → conversations. Current state: 4–6 leads/month against a need of ~12 qualified conversations/month (33% close rate, 4 closes/month founder capacity = ~48/year). Nehal is running at ~40% of his own closing throughput — doubling leads is the cheapest capacity unlock in the model.

Channels, ranked: ads, cold email, content, referral, partners, speaking, podcasts. Ads and cold email both depend on the same missing packaging. Pulse/AdScan spend-detection makes the outbound list literally the ICP screen run as a query — a capability almost no competing agency has. One forced referral experiment is owed: brands you made $2M/month for, plus hundreds of commission-aligned creators, all know DTC founders.

Intake criteria (qualification screen)

  1. Total monthly revenue — min $250K, ideally $1M+/month
  2. Current monthly spend — min $100K, ideally $1M/month
  3. Reachable targets — aggressively low ROAS / high CPA targets that allow testing. The stealth predictor: it measures whether the brand understands creative testing economics. Pass 1–2 to get the meeting; score 3 to set tier and terms.

Intro offer — the wedge

3 UGC videos, client pays only the $300 creator fee. Ad Pros finds creators, handles legal, writes scripts (or client provides). No product shipping — green-screen and talking-head only. Gated behind the ICP screen, not before it. It converts the sale from "trust me" to "here's your product working," and tests the client's approval speed live before any contract. Judged on 48–72h leading indicators (hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPM stability) with a spend floor per test ad — never "Meta didn't spend," never waiting weeks.

9. Creator marketplace

50Creators per brand minimum
3,000Creator slots at 60 clients
~2,100Unique creators after 30% overlap
~170/moNew creators needed at 8% creator churn

Creators are a full actor class in the performance system with their own funnel, win rates, and LTV — not a resource pool. Commission (50% of the rev share) is the loyalty mechanism: a proven creator routed across 2–3 non-competing brands earns more through Ad Pros than any single direct deal, so Ad Pros briefs jump their queue. Steady state is a barbell roster: a proven, heavily-reused core plus constant fresh-face intake for fatigue rotation and new verticals. The proven-creator roster is the durable asset of the business — brands churn; the data on what wins, the roster of who wins, and the routing that matches them is what a competitor can't copy.

10. OKR cascade for the performance system

  1. North Star: $30M/month creator-engine attributed revenue (net $1.5M/month)
  2. Survival OKR: time-to-$100K-attributed < 90 days per client (kills the kill zone)
  3. Growth OKRs per client: % of spend captured × attributed ROAS
  4. Engine OKR: winners per 100 videos — tracked per creator, per vertical, per format
  5. Pipeline OKR: 12 qualified conversations/month (packaging → channels → conversations)
  6. Retention OKR: monthly churn 8% → 5%; client approval latency as the leading indicator
  7. Roster OKRs: creator utilization (brands per proven creator), creator retention, recruitment throughput

11. Pre-mortem — what kills it

1. Creators don't perform — win rate per video too low. The core delivery bet. Mitigation: casting spec + Pulse pattern data + proven-core routing; track winners-per-100-videos from day one. Owner: Omar.
2. Clients take it in-house — success-driven churn, concentrating in the best accounts. Mitigation: the roster is Ad Pros' (non-circumvention + rev-share loyalty), pricing below in-house fully-loaded cost, and a designed "graduation" path — going in-house with Ad Pros creators licensed at a lower fee beats a clean loss. Owner: Nehal/Danna.
3. Creators get too expensive — the 50/50 split's success paradox; top earners field direct offers. Mitigation: multi-brand routing pays them more in aggregate than any defector deal — which only works if creator-level utilization tracking works. Owner: Daniela.
4. Packaging never ships — delivery eats the calendar and the Q3 constraint never resolves; pipeline stays at 4–6 leads/month. Mitigation: the sales kit above, built on a 2-week MVP clock (one-pager + case study + cold email first). Owner: Nehal.
5. The kill zone eats the book — time-to-$100K stays over 90 days and churn holds at 8%+, forcing a permanent replacement treadmill. Mitigation: survival OKR + SLA-gated delivery + disqualifying harder at intake. Owner: Danna (interim) → retention hire.
