Which to use for what

A practical split.

Read: Use First Click to judge prospecting creative, cold angles, new concept tests, and the top of the funnel — anything whose job is to find a customer who did not know Luhxe existed. Scoring both on one model will always overfund one half of the account. The tier ladder in this report applies to the first group.

Caveats

Read before acting on any single cut.

Launch cohort omitted: 81 of 100 ads report a first event date of Jul 26 — the first day of the window. The field is censored at the window boundary, not a real launch date, so a cohort cut would be a false signal. Needs a longer lookback.

Fulfillment cost missing: shipping, handling and fulfillment all read $0 in Triple Whale. Contribution margin computes to 77.3% of gross sales, putting breakeven NC ROAS at 1.29×. If fulfillment runs 8–12% of net sales — normal for apparel at a $204 AOV — true breakeven moves to 1.45–1.52×, and the 1.50× floor becomes a breakeven line rather than a success line.

Model: everything here is First Click on a lifetime window — see the Model Contrast tab, which is not optional reading. The same 30 days under Triple Attribution put Meta at 1.95× and $117.78 CPA; under Northbeam Clicks Only / 7-day, 1.09× and $207 CAC.
