Nurse Babes Creator Plan v1

Strategist: Adrienne · Drafted May 21, 2026 · 90-day execution window
Ad Pros Creator Operations

Executive Summary

Nurse Babes is targeting $1M/month in Meta spend, with 50% ($500k) driven by creator content. To get there, the brand needs 25 meaningful active creators producing on a steady cadence and 10-20 new creators tested every month to keep the funnel alive.

That ambition exists today — but the operating scaffold doesn't. Two creator platforms (Trybe + Social Snowball) run in parallel with no defined ownership, retainer offers vary case-by-case, and there's no published KPI framework. This plan fixes that.

This is the v1 operating blueprint. It maps two-platform roles, retainer SKU standardization, KPIs, dashboards, operating cadence, and a 90-day rollout. It also ties the creator pipeline directly to the Comfort Club ambassador layer — the long-term flywheel.

How this plan works — division of labor.

Ad Pros provides the structure: strategy, frameworks, briefs, performance analysis, and creator-tier definitions. The Nurse Babes team executes: daily submission review, compliance checks, brief distribution to creators on Trybe / Discord / Snowball, Tier 3 creator outreach + onboarding, and day-to-day creator relationship management.

This plan is the operating scaffold that the Nurse Babes team runs against. Ad Pros doesn't have a Creator Lead role — the day-to-day creator management is the client's team's responsibility. Ad Pros provides the strategic direction and the artifacts (briefs, scripts, KPI framework, recommendations) that make the execution scalable.

3. Two-Platform Operating Model

The fix: Trybe and Social Snowball are not competitors — they own different jobs. Stop running them in parallel with no division of labor. Lock the roles below.

Trybe → Paid-Performance UGC

Owns: Production of creator-led UGC content explicitly intended to power paid ad accounts.

Mechanic: 10% of creator earnings on flat-fee / retainer, OR 1.5% of Trybe-attributed sales.

Integration: Direct Shopify sync + Meta Partnership Ads launch from approved submissions in one click.

Best for: Feeding the 25-30 NEW concepts/month testing volume. Whitelisted Meta ads at scale.

Decision rule: If the content is going to be a Meta ad, route through Trybe.

Social Snowball → Affiliate, Referral, Ambassador

Owns: Customer-to-affiliate conversion, TikTok Shop creator tracking, Comfort Club ambassador layer.

Mechanic: $199/mo + 3% of affiliate-generated revenue.

Integration: Shopify-native, TikTok Shop tracking, UGC media library that auto-saves affiliate-tagged posts.

Best for: The "every-customer-is-a-potential-affiliate" model post-purchase. Comfort Club Month-6 ambassador unlock.

Decision rule: If the content is going to drive organic reach + attribution-trackable referrals, route through Social Snowball.

6. Brief Distribution & Approval Pipeline

Pipeline Stages

  1. Brief drafted by Ad Pros (from the Tier 1 brief library — NB-A01 through NB-A24)
  2. Brief distributed to assigned creators by the client's team via Trybe / Discord / Snowball
  3. Creator submits first cut within 7 days (SLA)
  4. Client's team reviews for brand voice + compliance + format within 48 hours (using Ad Pros-provided compliance framework + Schedule A vocabulary lock)
  5. Approved: Whitelist via Trybe → Meta Partnership Ads → live within 24 hours (Ad Pros executes the paid scale)
  6. Revision-requested: Specific feedback (using Ad Pros-provided revision template) + 5-day SLA for revised cut
  7. Declined: Counts against creator's strike record. 3 strikes within 90 days = demoted from active roster.
Ad Pros artifacts the client's team uses: compliance framework (Schedule A), revision template, approval criteria checklist, kill criteria thresholds. Ad Pros provides them once and updates as needed; client's team operates against them daily.

Approval Criteria

  • Brand voice: Matches the locked vocabulary from Step 5 ("show up strong" / cloud-comfort / nurse-tribe). Avoids the locked exclusion list ("pain-free" / "treats" / "cures" / "medical-grade" / "Trusted Solution for All Foot Conditions").
  • Compliance: No FTC-prohibited health claims (per Gravity Defyer ruling Feb 2025). Customer testimonial quotes exempt as third-party voice.
  • Format: Native to placement — 9:16 for Meta Reels / TikTok / IG Stories. 4:5 secondary. 1:1 for Google.
  • Hook within 3 seconds: First three seconds must carry the hook (per AC148 lesson — engagement-bait formats fail at TOF).
  • Usage rights confirmed: Perpetual paid-media usage rights signed before content goes live (lesson from At Home Dog Review loss).

Kill Criteria

Ad spend on a single creator's content kills when:

  • CPA > account CPA × 1.5 over 7 days at ≥ $500 spend, OR
  • ROAS < 1.5 over 7 days at ≥ $500 spend, OR
  • Compliance flag raised by Meta (any creative-side restriction).

8. Compliance & Contracts

Hard rule (from the At Home Dog Review loss): No content goes live without perpetual paid-media usage rights signed in the creator contract. The $1M-revenue Dog Review ad was killed because rights expired. This will not happen again.

Standard Contract Terms (every creator)

  1. Perpetual paid-media usage rights on all delivered + raw/unselected footage. No 12-month expiry.
  2. Whitelist / Partnership Ads handle rights for Meta + TikTok. Creator stays as the publisher of record.
  3. FTC disclosure compliance — #ad / #sponsored required on all paid placements.
  4. Brand exclusivity — no direct nurse-footwear competitor work for the contract term + 60 days post.
  5. Termination clause — 30-day notice from either side. Content remains usable post-termination per perpetual rights clause.
  6. Revenue share terms (Tier 1 only) — 10% of attributed sales for 12 months from content launch.

FTC + Meta Compliance Checklist (applied to every submission)

  • No "pain-free / treats / cures / heals / fixes / medical-grade / clinically proven / plantar fasciitis cure"
  • No "Trusted Solution for All Foot Conditions" or unnamed-orthopedist claims
  • Customer testimonial quotes exempt as third-party voice (per Step 1 vocabulary lock)
  • FTC disclosure visible within first 3 seconds for video, on-image for static
  • No competitor brand name comparisons without clean factual basis
