What happened

Seven new creatives launched Monday; three quiz concepts plus one iteration Thursday.

Why

Both funnels gave back ground: new ads in learning, product-page order value down. Breakdown below.

Spend

Eased to $22.3k from $24.7k. Quiz is ~22% of Meta spend vs the 60% target; the June 4 launches are still picking up budget. We keep pulling from legacy non-quiz campaigns.

Why NC ROAS is down

The gap in one number: cost per new customer

Early May: ~$76 per new customer. Now: $107. At ~$70 first-order value, 90% needs ~$78; the quiz is the only funnel near that line.

The engine that bought customers at $84 came down

Ads built on Emory's content bought customers at ~$84 in their prime and carried up to half of Meta spend. They came down May 18–28 over usage rights, and the budget moved to ads buying at $118–135. That swap explains roughly 60% of Meta's decline since mid-May; the rest is the surviving ads aging at the same time.

New ads are in learning

11 new creatives launched June 1–4. Quiz cost per customer rose from $74 to $97 as its spend nearly doubled; new ads start expensive, then settle.

Product-page order value fell

Cost per customer held at $130, but first-order value dropped from $72 to $64: same spend, less revenue back.

Quiz is still only ~21% of Meta spend

The strongest funnel (69% vs 49% NC ROAS, Triple Attribution) doesn't carry enough budget to lift the blend yet. The shift added ~3 points.

What we think this means

The causes are known and the fixes are live

More quiz budget is the fastest path toward 90%. New quiz creatives go live June 11; optimized results pages June 12.

Meta is re-benchmarked

The old 55% Meta target fit a mix where Google and organic carried more of the load. The new analysis, anchored to May 1–17 when the business ran 92%, resets the Meta hurdles to 77% on the quiz and 79% on non-quiz (Triple Attribution). At the current spend mix, those are the numbers that add up to 90% business-wide.

Meta · next steps

Hold Meta spend while business-wide NC ROAS is under 90%

We lift spend once the quiz shift and new pages bring it back.

Keep shifting budget to the quiz

Target is 60/40. Three more quiz ads in development.

Keep new concepts flowing into testing

Seven launched June 1, four more June 4. Fresh angles stay first.
