    How NC-ROAS is calculated

    NC-ROAS = order value ÷ cost per new customer.

    This is new-customer revenue from Shopify divided by paid spend (excluding Amazon). At the latest average order value of $69.20, the cost to acquire a new customer needs to be $76.89 or lower to hit the 0.90x goal.

    Method

    Method and constraints.

    • Benchmark period: May 1–17, 2026, the sub-monthly window where no-Amazon Shopify NC-ROAS was above target at 0.92x.
    • Latest complete week: June 3–9, 2026, in shop-local dates. June 10 is not used because it is the current partial day.
    • Business metric: Shopify new-customer order revenue from orders_table divided by no-Amazon paid spend from blended_stats_tvf(include_amazon=FALSE).
    • Attribution metric: Pixel new-customer order revenue / spend from pixel_joined_tvf(sales_platform_filter='shopify'), lifetime window, model-labeled as Triple Attribution, Linear All, or Linear Paid.
    • Meta split: campaign, ad set, or ad name contains “quiz” = Meta quiz; remaining Facebook Ads = Meta non-quiz.
    • Feasibility caps: no operating benchmark is allowed above that channel/sub-channel’s best trailing-12-month 30-day actual in the same attribution model.
    • LTV not used: Recent 30-day LTV is circular for customers acquired inside the same 30-day window, so benchmarks are based on first-purchase NC-ROAS only.
    • Amazon: excluded from no-Amazon Shopify NC-ROAS benchmarks unless Amazon revenue is added to the numerator.
