What happened

●

Spend held flat at $41K (+1% WoW)

Budget was essentially unchanged week-over-week, so movement is efficiency, not scale.

●

NC ROAS fell to 0.55 (−24%)

New-customer orders dropped 438 → 322 and NC revenue fell $29K → $22K.

●

NC CPA rose to $125.91 (+37%)

We paid more per new customer for fewer of them, even though CTR actually ticked up to 4.8%.

Why it happened AI draft

●

Engagement held, conversion didn't

CTR rose to 4.8% (+7%) while NC ROAS fell. Top-of-funnel interest was fine; the drop is downstream at conversion, not the hook.

●

Spend concentrated in mid-ROAS video

C018 (TTS breakout-winners video) absorbed ~$4.6K at 0.66 NC ROAS. Volume shifted toward proven video but at efficiency below the account target.

●

Cause under investigation

Tying the conversion-rate drop to a specific campaign, audience, or LP change needs the growth marketer's account view. Flagged, not assumed.

What we learned AI draft

CTR is not the bottleneck this week

Creative is still earning the click. Optimization focus should move to post-click: offer, landing page, and audience quality.

Flat spend masked a volume drop

Holding budget kept the topline steady but NC orders fell 26%. Watch order volume, not just spend, as the pacing signal.
