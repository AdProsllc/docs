What happened

We scaled Meta spend back about 20% and grouped the strongest Serum and 2-Step ads into a Max Value setup. Budget moved toward Cost Cap and Max Value, away from the weaker Max Conversions tests.

Why

The earlier dip came from conversion rate, not click cost. Which ads and funnels get the budget is the real lever. Conversion rate is now up 42% week on week, so the pullback and the shift are working.

Spend

Held back on purpose while we prove the recovery. We turn it up once NC ROAS climbs back toward target and the new UGC creative is tested.

What we think this means

The earlier drop was a creative and conversion problem, not traffic

Click costs and click-through held steady while conversion rate fell, so the fix is better ads and tighter funnel choices, not cheaper clicks. Higher order value helps the ROAS math too.

Serum PDP is the steadiest area to lean on

Serum holds the best relative NC ROAS across the account. We are shifting confidence back to Serum winners for the short term while the quiz creative is rebuilt.

The quiz is still the right direction, but it needs new creative first

The weak spot was the ads, not the funnel. We are keeping quiz budget low until the new real-person UGC concepts are produced and tested, then we judge the quiz on fair creative.

Meta · next steps

Hold Meta spend steady while business-wide NC ROAS is under 90%

Meta is the scale channel. We turn it up once conversion and the new creative prove out.

Concentrate budget on Serum PDP winners and Max Value

Max Value is the stronger test once the weak ad sets are removed. Serum PDP is the best relative performer, so it gets the confidence now.

Get the new UGC creative into testing

A few ads clear the bar, but the pool is thin and leans on one format. Fresh real-person angles widen the winners as spend grows.
