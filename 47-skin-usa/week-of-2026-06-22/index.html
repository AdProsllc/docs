On the testing groups the broken input is click-through: cheaper impressions, equal or better conversion, fine order value, but clicks cost 2 to 3 times more. The proven group has the opposite gap: it wins clicks at 2.12% but converts them at 1.2%, capping it in the mid-40s. NC CR is TripleWhale orders over Meta link clicks.

What we think this means

The gap is creative strategy, and every group shows a piece of it

The testing groups convert but can’t win the click; the proven ads win the click but convert at 1.2%, under what 90% needs. No group does both.

Authority-style single-creator UGC is the rebuild, and it starts now

It is the only format that has won the click and the conversion together, and none has run since those ads came down. Six scripts are with your team; mashup, animation and AI-voiceover iterations are done.

New quiz results pages are live, with no clean read yet

Live June 26; the days since ran through the sale and the weekend drop, so quiz numbers show no signal yet. We have Octane access and run the page analysis ourselves next week.
