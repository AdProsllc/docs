What happened

●

Spend rose at $40K (+28% WoW)

Budget was increased into the channel this week.

●

NC ROAS fell to 0.72 (−6% WoW)

New-customer orders went 368 → 438 and NC revenue $24K → $29K.

●

NC CPA rose to $91.83 (+7% WoW)

We paid more per new customer for fewer of them.

Why it happened

●

Engagement softened, conversion lagged

CTR fell to 4.47% while NC ROAS fell. Top and bottom of funnel moved together this week.

●

Spend concentrated in proven video

C018 (TTS breakout-winners) absorbed the largest share at 0.72 NC ROAS — volume leaned on known winners.

●

Cause to confirm in-account

Tie the conversion movement to a specific campaign, audience, or LP change. Flagged, not assumed.

What we learned

Watch creative fatigue

Engagement softened — refresh the top-of-funnel creative rotation.

Track volume, not just spend

Flat spend can mask a 19% move in NC orders. Order volume is the better pacing signal.

Meta — next week

1

Audit the post-click path on top video

Pull LP + checkout conversion for C018 (TTS breakout-winners) traffic to locate the drop.

2

Rebalance toward efficient angles

Shift budget out of sub-target-ROAS variants into the angles holding above account target.

3

Hold spend until efficiency recovers

No Meta scale next week until NC ROAS recovers toward 0.7+.

What happened

●

Spend held roughly flat at $4K (−10% WoW)

Budget moved little week-over-week, so the story is efficiency rather than scale.

●

NC ROAS improved to 2.48 (+39% WoW)

New-customer orders went 116 → 136 and NC revenue $9K → $11K.

●

NC CPA improved to $31.81 (−24% WoW)

Acquisition got cheaper this week.

Why it happened

●

Prior week sets the baseline

Google's NC ROAS norm sits ~1.5–1.8; this week ran above trend. Part of the swing is mean-reversion.

●

Query mix matters

A shift between branded and non-branded prospecting moves CPA and NC ROAS. Worth confirming in-account.

What we learned

Google is the efficiency anchor

At 2.48 NC ROAS it remains the most efficient channel. There may be room to grow its share of budget.

Google — next week

1

Test a controlled budget lift

Google is under-allocated relative to its efficiency. Trial a 15–20% increase and watch NC CPA.

2

Segment branded vs non-branded

Report the two separately so efficiency swings are explainable.
