    The drop is a conversion rate problem, not a traffic cost one. Serum PDP is the one funnel still holding NC ROAS.

    What happened

    The drop was conversion rate, not traffic cost

    • NC ROAS fell from 0.35x to 0.26x against the 0.60x benchmark.
    • NC CPA rose 38%, from $235 to $324.
    • Traffic inputs held: CPM, CTR, CPC, new-visitor rate and NC AOV were all flat or better.
    • The single driver was new customer conversion rate, down 26% (2.62% to 1.95%).

    Why

    2-Step took a big share of spend at weak efficiency

    • Spend share jumped from 5.4% to 39.9% by design, to test it.
    • It converted weak: NC CR 1.49%, NC CPA $535, NC ROAS 0.23x.
    • So a large slice of budget went to the account's weakest funnel during the week.

    Quiz was the worst on both the click and the conversion

    • Weak click: 0.51% CTR on 225k impressions, vs 1.61% for Serum PDP. The ads barely earn the click.
    • Weak conversion: the ~860 link clicks it did get produced only 13 new customers.
    • Result: NC CR 0.70%, NC CPA $813, NC ROAS 0.04x.
    • The June 29 Quiz creatives all failed. Spend share was cut from 30.9% to 17.3%.

    Serum PDP held while everything else dropped

    • Best CTR of the three funnels at 1.61%, and the most new customers at 127.
    • NC CPA stayed stable ($195 to $201); NC ROAS held around 0.39x.
    • Still below the 0.60x goal, but clearly the strongest funnel and the base to rebuild on.

    What we think this means

    The Quiz issue is a creative problem, not a funnel problem

    • Quiz conversion returned to normal once the end-of-month sale ended, so the funnel is not broken.
    • The weak input is the creative: low CTR up front, weak conversion after the click.
    • Keeping Quiz budget low until the new UGC concepts are live and tested.

    Max Value beats Max Conversions on 2-Step, once BestPDP is excluded

    • Clean 2-Step read: Max Value 0.38x NC ROAS vs Max Conversions 0.20x, with far higher CR (7.41% vs 0.88%) and AOV ($171 vs $125).
    • Why BestPDP is excluded: it ran 0.19x in Max Conv the prior week, was relaunched into Max Value last week, and failed again at 0.26x. It's a known dud dragging the raw comparison.
    • Max Value buys pricier traffic but the conversion quality offsets it. Still below 0.60x, so a stronger test, not a scale-ready winner.

    One Serum PDP ad is already hitting the goal

    • Above $600 spend last 7 days, one active ad is at or above 0.60x: a Serum PDP ad at 0.73x NC ROAS, $112 NC CPA, 13.3% NC CR.
    • Other Serum PDP leaders are strong relative performers but still below goal.

    Meta · next steps

    Scaled Meta budget back 20%

    Done. Cut spend on the weak 2-Step and Quiz tests and shifted to the Serum PDP winners. Early result: the last 3 days recovered to 59% NC ROAS on lower daily spend, with July 8 at 79%. Holding the lower budget until it clears 90%.

    Get the new UGC creative live

    The authority-style UGC rebuild is the core fix for the conversion problem. Priority is producing and launching it, then testing hook rate and CTR.

    Test the Serum funnel on Max Value

    Max Value is the stronger 2-Step optimization once BestPDP is excluded. Extend the read to the Serum funnel to confirm it.
