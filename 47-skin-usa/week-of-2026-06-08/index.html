The quiz problem is the click, not the page: similar NC AOV and a higher NC CVR, but a 0.67% CTR drives CPC to $2.87 and starves the funnel. Neither segment is at target yet; Non-Quiz is the stronger of the two.

What happened

Meta NC ROAS fell to 44% last week, down from 55%. The new June quiz concepts we leaned into didn't hold up. Over the last few days we paused the weakest quiz adsets and pulled budget back toward product-page ads. Spend held roughly flat.

Why

The new quiz creative ran cold. Click-through on the June quiz concepts came in around 0.58% versus 1.45% in May, and cost per click roughly doubled, so those adsets ran well under target. Non-Quiz is holding up better, so we're shifting weight back to product-page ads while we rework the quiz.

Spend

Held roughly flat, not scaling, while the quiz creative is reworked and the stronger Non-Quiz ads carry the weight.

Quiz creative · what didn't work, and what's next

The new June quiz concepts averaged a 0.58% click-through rate against a 1.45% baseline. That 60% drop in clicks roughly doubled CPC from $1.51 to $3.04, which pulled ncROAS down across the batch. The pattern is consistent: each concept moves from hook to quiz too fast, with no emotional middle, so the customer never feels heard enough to click.

C068 · the one to build on

Best performer. A 25% hook rate and 7.32% new-customer conversion show the "adult breakouts are different" angle lands. It still routes to the quiz before the customer is invested, so we're iterating on it with a stronger hook and a longer format.

C059 · right message, wrong format

The transformation angle works (4.95% new-customer conversion), but the before/after stills triggered Meta review limits and scroll-away. We're testing the same "4 weeks" message in video without the before/after stills.

C054 · strong hook, too short

The hook works (19% hook rate) but it cuts to the quiz with no transformation visual or emotional depth, so it's too short to build investment. Reworking with a longer, deeper middle.

C058 · format mismatch

Too text-heavy, and the model already has clear skin so there's no identification moment. Static format is the wrong fit for the quiz funnel. Pausing for quiz.

C070 · weak hook

Low hook rate (8.8–10%) and vague messaging, no emotional resonance. Pausing for quiz.

C071 · wrong funnel

The Silver Chitoderm education belongs on the product page, where it runs a 2.74x ROAS, not in the quiz. Redirecting it to the product page.

What we're doing differently: extending videos to 25–30s with real emotional pain-point depth before routing to the quiz; iterating on C068; testing C059's "4 weeks" message in video; moving Silver Chitoderm education to the product page; and studying two brands running strong quiz funnels on Meta to shape the next batch. Targets: 1.5%+ CTR, under $2 CPC, 2% new-customer conversion, 0.70 ncROAS.

What we think this means

Non-Quiz is the stronger funnel right now

Non-Quiz ran 52% NC ROAS over the last 3 days vs 32% for quiz, on a much better click (2.03% CTR vs 0.67%). We're shifting weight there in the short term to steady NC ROAS while the quiz creative gets reworked. To be clear, it's the stronger of the two, not at target yet.

Image ads are converting new customers better than video right now

Across both funnels, images are converting new customers at a higher rate than video but barely getting any spend. On the quiz, images ran 0.47 NC ROAS last week vs 0.21 for video; outside the quiz, images converted new customers at 6.67% vs 0.57% for video. We're putting more test budget behind images to see if that holds as spend grows.

Meta · next steps

Hold Meta spend steady while business-wide NC ROAS is under 90%

Meta is the scale channel. Lift it once NC ROAS recovers and the next creative batch proves out.

Shift weight to the stronger Non-Quiz ads

Concentrate budget on the Non-Quiz product-page ads carrying the better ncROAS in the short term, and rework the quiz creative before re-funding it.

Rework the quiz creative around the hook

Longer videos that build the pain point before the quiz ask, plus more image concepts. Targets: 1.5%+ CTR, under $2 CPC.
