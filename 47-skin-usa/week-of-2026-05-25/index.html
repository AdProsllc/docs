What happened

Meta NC ROAS held flat at 55% week-on-week. The Emori creator ads (50–60% of Meta spend through mid-May) were turned off, and budget reallocated to other ad concepts to fill the gap.

Why

The Emori ads ran ~75% NC ROAS at their best and were sliding (0.77 to 0.51). Their replacements land ~55% NC ROAS, where Meta sits overall. No single ad concept is beating the account average, and ads sending traffic to the quiz convert better than ads sending to the product page.

Spend

Held roughly steady on purpose. We turn it up once ROAS recovers and the new creative and quiz funnel prove out.

What we think this means

The quiz funnel is outperforming the standard product page

Emori ran 51% NC ROAS last week vs 56% for the rest of Meta: a big share of spend, but no stronger than average, and her replacements land about the same. No ad concept is pulling ahead. The clearer driver is where the ad sends people. In the same campaign last week, adsets routing to the quiz ran 82% NC ROAS vs 54% for adsets routing to the standard product page (same creative, different destination). Both better creative and the quiz destination are levers.

US AOV is about double the UK, worth protecting

Why bundles and 2-step matter here: they lean into a higher-value customer, which helps the ROAS math while cost per customer is high.

Meta · next steps

Hold Meta spend steady while business-wide ROAS is under 90%

Meta is the scale channel. Lift it once creative and the quiz funnel strengthen and the business is back at target.

Shift budget toward the quiz funnel

The quiz runs 82% NC ROAS vs 54% to the standard page in the same campaign. We recommend trimming non-quiz spend and concentrating budget on the quiz path. $400 daily minimum is set; Enchant is building the four results pages, landing June 12.

Get more new creative concepts into testing

No current concept beats the ~55% account average, so the priority is producing and testing fresh angles, not iterating on the existing batch.
